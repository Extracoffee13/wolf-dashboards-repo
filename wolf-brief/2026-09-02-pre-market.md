# WOLF Pre-Market — 2026-09-02

**Oil, yields, and the Fed are finally pulling the same direction, and it isn't bullish.**

- Fresh US-Iran strikes overnight pushed Brent near $95 and the 10-year to 4.81% — the highest since November 2023 — while Fed Governor Barr is out saying she'd back a hike if inflation doesn't cool. War-driven inflation meeting a Fed willing to hike into it is the setup that actually hurts multiples, not the headline risk everyone's already numb to.
- **Watch SPX 7,600.** It closed Tuesday at 7,686, right at the top of a 7,600–7,700 support band. Lose 7,600 and the next real floor is 7,560, where a 50-day moving average and a Gann confluence line up — that's the level, not a round number.
- Kospi closed **-4%** overnight, worse than the oil story alone explains — that's yen/BOJ-hike risk bleeding into Asia, and it's the tell that this isn't just a Middle East story anymore.
- ADP printed a soft **+38K vs. +47K expected** this morning, the weakest since January. That sets up Friday's NFP as the actual fight: a bad number forces the market to pick between "Fed cuts on weak labor" and "Fed hikes on war inflation" — it can't have both, and Friday likely decides which.
- On single names: **NVDA +8.9%** premarket on a JPMorgan data-center demand upgrade ($38.2B FY27 estimate, Blackwell ~80% allocated) and **GTLB +20%** on a clean beat-and-raise are the only stories today untouched by the macro overhang — worth noting who's still buying growth into a rising-rate tape.

**What would change my view:** a ceasefire or de-escalation headline out of the Iran conflict. That reverses oil and yields together and this whole setup unwinds fast — it's the single biggest risk to being cautious here.

# WOLF Brief — Consulting Pulse
**Date:** June 23, 2026 | **Routine:** consulting-pulse

---

## The Surprise

Bain just quantified exactly how much harder PE has gotten, and the number is brutal: a deal that needed 5% EBITDA growth to generate a 2.5x return a decade ago now requires **12% EBITDA growth** to hit the same target. That's not a market cycle — it's a structural shift in the return math that most mid-market GPs haven't internalized yet. Bain's June midyear report calls this out in the context of a "triple shock" that killed PE's second consecutive revival attempt: the AI-driven SaaSpocalypse in software (tech deal value down 70% in one quarter), private credit redemption stress, and the Iran conflict's oil price spike.

The embedded insight most readers will miss: **AI is no longer optional in PE value creation — it's the only path to clearing the new 12% hurdle**. Bain says explicitly that "inaction on AI is now a strategic choice, not a neutral decision." That's consultant-speak for *you're already behind if you're still evaluating*.

---

## The Second Paper Worth Your Attention

BCG published a real estate AI brief in the same window that puts a number on what AI-first developers can capture: **400–700 basis points of margin uplift** from AI applied to construction management, procurement, and scheduling — plus ~30% compression in project timelines. The framing is urgent: BCG argues there is a "narrow window" before AI becomes table stakes in real estate development, meaning the firms that move now build a durable structural advantage, and the ones that wait get commoditized.

For FL homebuilders and anyone touching the Brand 9 client base, this is the business case document for the AI conversation you've been trying to initiate.

---

## Why Most Readers Haven't Seen This Yet

The Bain midyear report dropped on June 8 and got buried in the noise of the Iran conflict coverage. The number everyone quoted was "PE recovery stalled" — nobody led with the return math restructuring or the AI-inaction framing. The BCG real estate piece hasn't surfaced outside PropTech circles because it's technically an "Executive Perspectives" brief rather than a full report, so it doesn't hit the normal distribution channels.

## If I'm Wrong

**Falsifiable prediction, to be graded September 23, 2026:**

The Bain 12% EBITDA growth threshold will NOT become the consensus benchmark for PE underwriting because inflation moderates faster than expected, the Fed cuts accelerate, and compressed multiples give way to an exit window re-opening — making the old 5% math viable again for smaller deals below $250M. If deal volume in sub-$250M buyouts increases by more than 15% in Q3 2026 vs. Q2, this prediction holds and the Bain thesis is overstated for the mid-market.

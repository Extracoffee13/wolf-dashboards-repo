# WOLF Pre-Market — Monday, August 24, 2026

**The 10-year is running the show this week, not the White House.** Yields near 4.72%, close to a 20-month high, and that's what's leaning on Nasdaq futures (-0.4%ish) harder than the S&P (-0.2%ish) or the Dow this morning — not the Canada tariff headline everyone's talking about.

- **Watch the 10-year against 4.75%.** Clean break above and long-duration tech gets another leg of pressure into Nvidia's Wednesday print; a fade back under 4.65% takes the pressure off and NDX outperforms into Jackson Hole.
- **US-Canada trade war is loud, not the driver.** 50% tariffs on ~$20B of Canadian goods went live at midnight Saturday, Canada retaliates September 8 — real, but small-cap/industrials-level noise, not an index-mover. Don't let the headline distract from the rate story.
- **Wednesday, Aug 26, after the close: NVDA reports.** ~$93-95B revenue expected, ~96% y/y growth priced in. This is the week's real equity catalyst — everything before it is positioning.
- **Friday, Aug 28, 10am ET: Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole keynote as Fed Chair**, 19 days ahead of the September 16 FOMC. That's the actual reason yields are elevated into this week, not the tariff news.
- **No homebuilder earnings today** — Toll Brothers already printed (Aug 19, beat but decelerating y/y) and that's the freshest read on builder health until the next batch. Not a today story.

**What would change my view:** a 10-year print back under 4.60% before Wednesday — that would flip the NDX-underperformance thesis and argue the market's already pricing a dovish Warsh, in which case Nvidia becomes the whole week's story again.

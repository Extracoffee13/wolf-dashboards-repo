# WOLF Brief — 2026-06-26 Pre-Market

*Public-facing. Opinionated. Five bullets. Receipts in wolf-intel/2026-06-26/pre-market.md.*

---

**Headline**: Micron's 4.5x YoY revenue print ($41.46B) is the session's real story — this is HBM demand confirmed, not narrativized. Trade the semis chain, not the AI hype cycle.

---

1. **MU is up 15% pre-market and that number is the brief.** Q3 FY2026: $41.46B revenue (was $9.3B a year ago), $25.11 EPS vs $20.20 expected, 83% cloud memory gross margin. Sixteen multi-year supply agreements with data center operators. This is not a beat — it's a structural regime change in AI memory demand. NQ opens at a 2.5-week high; Broadcom, AMAT, LRCX, and Nvidia all follow the tape. Watch NQ 2.5-week resistance — a close above it is the weekly confirmation signal.

2. **KBH +16%, LEN/DHI/PHM/TOL +7%+: Housing Act passage is the squeeze you were warned about.** The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act cleared Congress 85-5 in the Senate and 358-32 in the House — the largest housing supply bill in decades. Trump cancelled the signing ceremony (prefers SAVE America Act), so implementation risk is real but the legislative passage alone is the catalyst. Don't fade the homebuilder squeeze waiting for a Trump signature that may or may not come. Builders FirstSource (BLDR) +11% is the supply-chain tell that this is more than a sentiment pop.

3. **The Iran/Hormuz overnight spike was a head fake — for now.** Iran threatened to re-close the Strait following Israeli strikes on Hezbollah. WTI +2% at Asia open, then fully reversed. Crude is only 7% above pre-war levels vs the 50%+ peak in March. This tells you the market has (1) priced in a sustained Hormuz risk premium and (2) is now fading each flare unless the Strait physically closes. Don't short the oil reversal; don't chase it either. This is event-risk headline noise until the physical close, which would be a full regime change.

4. **PCE 4.1% headline / 3.4% core (May data, released yesterday): the Fed is locked.** These are the highest readings since April 2023 and October 2023 respectively. In-line with consensus, so no shock — but Iran energy costs embedding into the price level means no cut path opens before July FOMC at the earliest, and realistically not until energy inflation resolves. Bonds drift lower; the risk trade is equity-specific, not macro-driven.

5. **RTY 3,000 is tonight's structural close.** Today is the first-ever semi-annual Russell reconstitution close. Small caps are pinned to 3,000 — the psychological level and the rebalancing magnet. Expect elevated intraday volume and volatility. The post-close flow resets Monday's small-cap opening tone: a clean close above 3,000 is constructive; a failure below it heading into the weekend with Iran headline risk overhead is the setup for a gap-down open Monday.

---

**What would change my view**: If WTI reclaims $80 on a confirmed Hormuz physical closure, the entire risk-on stack (MU, housing, NQ) rolls over and the June rally reverses in a single session. The Iran headline risk is asymmetric to the downside and is not fully priced. Alternatively, if Trump signs the housing bill this week (reversed the cancellation), homebuilder puts are the wrong side and the sector trades to new cycle highs.

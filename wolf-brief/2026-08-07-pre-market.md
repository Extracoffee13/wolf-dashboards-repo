# WOLF Pre-Market — Friday, August 7, 2026

**Headline: Jobs report broke, and the market bet on a Fed that isn't ready to bend.** July payrolls fell 23,000 against a call for +83,000 — the first outright decline in months, with participation at a five-year low. Futures ripped on the "bad news is good news" reflex (SPX futures +0.2%, QQQ +0.5%), but rate-cut odds trackers still price ~89% chance of zero Fed cuts in 2026. That gap doesn't hold both ways for long.

- **Level to watch: SPX 7,620.** The index needs consecutive closes above it to confirm the bounce is real; reject it again and 7,300 is the next magnet. Range has been 7,223–7,653 since mid-May — today either breaks that or extends it.
- **Ad-tech is broken, not the market.** Trade Desk (TTD) is down ~27% pre-market on a guidance miss and a simultaneous CFO/CMO/commercial-chief shakeup — that's a company-specific crater, don't read it across growth names. Doximity (DOCS) +77% on beat-and-raise is the opposite signal sitting right next to it.
- **Oil is the real risk-off input, not jobs.** Brent +4% to ~$83 on US-Iran/Strait of Hormuz tension drove Thursday's Dow -0.85% drop. JPMorgan still sees this fading to $80 by Q4 — but until it does, every equity rally is trading with a geopolitical tax on it.
- **No FOMC today, but two Fed voices are on stage** — Bowman at 12:45pm ET, Cook at 4:05pm ET. Either one leaning dovish against the "no cuts" consensus is the surprise that moves the tape into the weekend.
- Homebuilders: nothing confirmed reporting today — most of that cohort already printed this cycle. Not today's story.

**What would change my view:** a close above SPX 7,620 held for two straight sessions, or a Fed speaker today opening the door to a September cut — either one flips this from "fragile bounce on bad data" to "real risk-on," and I'd rather be wrong early than late to that.

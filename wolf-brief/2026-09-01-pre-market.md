Don't fight the chip tape broadly; let the geopolitical shock wash through first. ISM Manufacturing at 10am ET (cons. 55.2) is the first real fundamental data point of the month — a soft print stacked on top of the Iran headline is what turns this from a one-day gap into a real drawdown.

Conversely, a sub-53 ISM Manufacturing print today turns this into a growth scare on top of a geopolitical one, and that's the combination that actually breaks the seasonal-weakness thesis open to the downside.

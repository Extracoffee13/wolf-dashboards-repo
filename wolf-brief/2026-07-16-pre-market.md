# Pre-Market — 2026-07-16

**Chips are eating a "sell the news" despite a blowout TSMC quarter, and it's hitting harder in Asia than in US futures — yet.**

- TSMC posted a record Q2 (net profit +77% YoY, revenue +36% YoY, AI chips = 61% of sales) and the stock fell anyway. That's not a fundamentals problem, it's an expectations problem — and it's dragging the whole chip complex. Kospi closed **-6.6%** (Bank of Korea rate hike piled on top), Nikkei **-2.9%** on Kioxia (-13.5%), SoftBank (-6.4%), Tokyo Electron (-5.2%). Hang Seng was the outlier, +1.7%.
- US futures are gapping down but selectively: **Nasdaq 100 -0.5% to -0.8%**, **S&P 500 -0.2%**, Dow roughly flat near 52,886, Russell 2000 -0.35%. Dow/small-caps shrugging it off tells you this is a chip-specific unwind, not broad risk-off — for now.
- **Watch NVDA/AMD/AVGO/MU at the open.** If US semis open down more than ~1-2% and don't bounce, the Asia move is importing directly and SPX likely tests **7,500** (S&P closed 7,572.40 July 15). If US chips shrug it off like the Dow futures are, this stays a Korea/Japan story and US dip-buyers step back in by midday.
- **PYPL is the tape's real mover**: +15-17% pre-market on a reported **$53B Stripe/Advent buyout bid at $60.50/share** (~28% premium). Board reportedly wants closer to $70. This is a live M&A story with real deal risk (financing, antitrust) — not a name to chase at the gap.
- Today's data (Retail Sales, Jobless Claims, Philly Fed) feeds directly into the live **September rate-hike** debate — yes, hike, not cut — currently priced around 60% after June CPI came in soft. A hot Retail Sales print reignites hike odds and could be the thing that actually breaks the "chips only" containment.

**What would change my view:** SPX closing back above 7,600 with the SOX green kills the sell-the-news thesis for now. A close below 7,500 with US semis down in sympathy with Korea/Japan says this spreads.

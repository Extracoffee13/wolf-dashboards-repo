FTSE closed (UK bank holiday), DAX barely holding +0.1%. The auto sector is the canary — tariff escalation has a further leg if EU retaliates. Short the sector or stay flat; no long thesis here.

The sector's affordability problem is now compounded by energy-driven rate pressure. XHB/ITB levels to watch: any break below recent lows with no Iran resolution is a short signal; DHI is the last one standing on scale if you need exposure.

**Watch:** SPX 7,200 — that's the first real support below current futures (7,253). A close below it on volume would confirm the oil/rate combo is starting to bite through the tech bid. **ISM Services Tuesday 10am ET** is the next catalyst: a sub-50 print ends the soft landing story.

**What would change my view:** A credible Hormuz ceasefire/reopening deal that takes oil back below $85 would immediately remove the stagflation tail, let the Fed pivot, re-rate homebuilders and consumer stocks, and validate the record highs. Until that headline prints, the bull case is tech-only and the rest of the market is living on borrowed time.

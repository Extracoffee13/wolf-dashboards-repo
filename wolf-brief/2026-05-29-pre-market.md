If 10Y reclaims 4.60%+, that is the circuit breaker for this ATH run.

The current equity ATH assumes Warsh holds rates at current levels and doesn't pivot hawkish. Any signal of a hike path or aggressive balance sheet tightening at the June meeting resets the entire tape. Small-cap RTY is already flat (-0.03% futures) while SPX hits ATHs — the bond market and the small-cap market are not fully bought in.

# WOLF Pre-Market — Aug 18, 2026

**Headline: Iran ceasefire lapsed, oil's bid, and the Nasdaq is paying for it.**

- Trump killed the extension on the 60-day Iran ceasefire and threatened to bomb Oman if it gets in the way of Hormuz — that's why NQ futures are down 1.3% overnight while the Dow's only off 0.1-0.2%. This is a growth-stock problem, not an index-wide panic.
- **Brent near $89, WTI ~$84, 10Y yield ~4.7%** — oil and yields are moving together, which is the stagflation trade, not the geopolitics trade. That's what's actually hitting Nasdaq multiples, not the headlines themselves.
- **FTSE 100 at 10,700 is the level.** It's the lowest since July 24 and DAX/Stoxx 50 are already at multi-day lows. A clean break below 10,700 in London says this leg has more room; holding it says Europe's done panicking for now.
- Home Depot beat ($47.86B rev vs $47.27B est) — housing-adjacent names get one data point of good news today, but no homebuilder earnings this week (PHM not until Oct 20) means Housing Starts/Permits/Pending Home Sales at 8:30/10:00 ET are the only builder-sector signal on the tape today.
- **Fed minutes are tomorrow, not today** — don't let anyone tell you CPI is a catalyst this morning, it already printed (3.4% y/y) and is stale. Nothing on today's calendar directly tests the "Fed is complacent on inflation" thesis currently driving yields — that test comes Wednesday.

**What would change my view:** a de-escalation headline out of Tehran or Washington on Hormuz access — that unwinds the oil bid, the yield move, and the Nasdaq underperformance in one shot, probably within the same session.

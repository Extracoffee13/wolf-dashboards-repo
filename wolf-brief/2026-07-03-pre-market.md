But Fed pricing for the July 29 meeting is ~89% hold, near-zero cut odds, with nine officials on record favoring a hike and 2026 inflation guidance lifted to 3.6%. The market is front-running a dovish Fed that isn't showing up in the dots.

- **Europe is running its own script:** Stoxx futures pushing record highs on a German coalition tax/labor/pension deal, not on the AI story. Don't conflate the European melt-up with US risk appetite — different driver, different durability.

**What would change my view:** if AMD/SMH open Monday and hold above Thursday's pre-selloff levels by midday, the AI-capex scare was a Korea-specific liquidity event, not a demand signal — and the semis short thesis dies fast.

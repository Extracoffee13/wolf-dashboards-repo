### The Surprise

M&A just dethroned operational improvement as the top value driver in private equity — and almost nobody in the operator class noticed because they were heads-down on AI pilots.

FTI Consulting surveyed 550+ senior PE leaders this week (released June 4). The finding that should stop you: **M&A went from dead last to #1** as a cited value driver in a single year (7% → 24% named it their top lever). At the same time, AI ROI is compressing faster than anyone modeled: **66% of PE firms see AI benefits within 12 months**. These two trends are not independent. The firms getting outsized returns are combining aggressive M&A with rapid AI deployment on the acquired asset — using AI to collapse the value creation timeline from a 3-year hold to something closer to 18 months.

The second paper nobody saw: **arxiv:2606.04306** (submitted June 3), from a team across McGill, Purdue, UNSW, UCLA, and NYU. It describes the exact architectural layer missing from every enterprise AI deployment right now — the "Organizational Control Layer" (OCL), a governance infrastructure that sits between an agent's proposed action and the real-world system it would affect. Without it, their tests showed unsafe executions in **88% of scenarios**. With it: near-zero unsafe executions, **96% valid success rate**. This is not theoretical. This is why PE firms can't scale their AI pilot programs — they have no OCL.

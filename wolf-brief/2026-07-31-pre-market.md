# WOLF Pre-Market Brief — July 31, 2026

**The AI-bubble scare was wrong, at least for one day.** Korea's KOSPI just posted its biggest single-day gain on record — +17.91% — completely erasing a three-day, 17% meltdown, after Samsung (+28%) and SK Hynix (+30%, limit-up) ripped on the back of Microsoft's Azure beat and Amazon's blowout AWS number. S&P futures are following, +0.5% to 7,508; Nasdaq-100 futures +1.1% to 28,552.

- **Watch NVDA/AMD at the open.** They're the direct US read-through to the Samsung/SK Hynix squeeze — if the semis rally holds past the first hour, it's real; if it fades by lunch, it was short-covering, not conviction.
- **AMZN +11–13% pre-market is the cleanest trade of the day** — AWS grew 37% y/y vs. 31% expected, fastest since 2021, and management guided capex to $220B. This is the number that flipped the entire overnight tape.
- **AAPL is the tell that not everyone gets a pass** — down 7.2% pre-market on a Services/China miss despite record iPhone-quarter revenue. ~$362B of market cap at risk. AI capex optimism didn't save a China miss.
- **The Fed is the landmine nobody's pricing today.** Three FOMC members (Hammack, Kashkari, Logan) dissented *hawkish* on Wednesday — they wanted to hike, not hold — and markets now put September hike odds at 63%. That's a very different macro backdrop than the AI-rally headlines suggest.
- **Energy earnings Friday**: XOM already missed (-2% pre-market, $3.52 vs. $3.60 est.) despite analysts expecting the best oil-company quarter in 15 quarters. CVX reports today too — a second miss would say oil-price tailwinds aren't converting to earnings across the group, not just at Exxon.

**Level to watch: SK Hynix/Samsung intraday follow-through.** If Korean chip names give back more than 5% of today's gain before the US close, this was a short squeeze, not a re-rate — fade the "AI bubble fears were overblown" narrative and expect NVDA/AMD to give back today's sympathy pop.

**What would change my view:** a hard reversal in Samsung/SK Hynix intraday, or a Chevron miss on top of Exxon's — either one says today's rally is earnings-season noise sitting on top of a genuinely hawkish Fed, not a real re-rate of the AI trade.

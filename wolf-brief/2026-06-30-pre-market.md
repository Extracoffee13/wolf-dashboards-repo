A July hike is live. The ceasefire-driven oil decline could cool headline PCE by Q3 — that's the bull thesis. But if Warsh moves before oil meaningfully cuts into core services, this rally reverses fast.

Fundamentally the business is fine (Azure +40%, AI at $37B run rate), but sentiment is broken. Watch $350 as the psychological floor. Short cover there is the trade.

KBH (June 23): revenue -27%, deliveries -23%, ASP $461,900. LEN (June 12): new orders -4%. The data is ugly. The one bright spot: KBH cancellation rate improved from 16% to 12% — demand destruction is slowing. The homebuilder trade (PHM, DHI, TOL) requires lower mortgage rates, which requires the ceasefire to hold oil down long enough to cool PCE. That's a two-step conditional that isn't confirmed yet. Don't chase the sector until the Fed signals pause.

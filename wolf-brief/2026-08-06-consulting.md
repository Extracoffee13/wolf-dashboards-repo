# The consulting firehose is mostly recycled water

Today's WOLF Consulting Pulse scanned ten strategy firms — McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, PwC, Strategy&, Oliver Wyman, Roland Berger — plus arXiv's cs.AI, econ.GN, and q-fin feeds, for anything genuinely new on AI agents, PE roll-ups, and family-office operations that would sharpen or kill a thesis we hold at The Construct.

**The surprise: there wasn't any.** Not "nothing good" — nothing *dated*. Every relevant piece the scan turned up — McKinsey's private-markets report on operational alpha replacing multiple expansion, Bain's midyear PE report on stalled 2026 dealmaking, BCG's operating-system-of-work piece on agent-ops ROI, KPMG's PE/AM AI-pulse survey — traces back weeks to months, not the last 48 hours. Meanwhile LinkedIn, SEO blogs, and consulting-adjacent newsletters make it look like a firehose of fresh agent-AI thinking is landing daily. It isn't. The real research cadence at these firms is quarterly and event-driven (Davos, midyear PE reports, quarterly AI pulse surveys), not a daily drip.

**What it means for The Construct:** the "expensive research nobody reads" arbitrage we're built on isn't just that the research is gated or dense — it's that most of what circulates as "new" thinking is repackaged quarterly output wearing a fresh publish date. A daily synthesis pass has more value catching the *quarterly* drops the day they land (PE reports, AI pulse surveys) than trying to mine a 48-hour window that's structurally close to empty most days. We should recalibrate this brief's cadence expectation accordingly rather than force-fitting a "paper of the day."

**If I'm wrong:** grade this in 90 days (by 2026-11-04). If, over the next quarter, MBB + Big 4 firms collectively ship more than 2 genuinely dated, agent-AI-or-PE-ops-relevant pieces per week on average — real new publish dates, not recycled Q1/Q2 2026 reports resurfacing in search — this "quarterly cadence, not daily drip" read is wrong, and the pulse should shift to a weekly rollup instead of a daily scan.

- **Florida HOA/sign law changes (effective July 1, 2026)** — https://www.floridacondohoalawblog.com/2026/07/28/the-hoa-architectural-control-statute-has-again-been-amended/
  - What happened: § 501.062 sets a statewide no-commercial-solicitation sign standard. § 720.3035 architectural-control rules were amended for exterior changes that need a building permit. The governor vetoed the Davie billboard bill (HB 4075) on Jun 25. HB 657 on HOA presuit mediation is pending in the Senate. No November FDOT change was found.
  - So what for Brand 9: HOA-facing monument and entry sign work should account for the revised architectural-control process. Verify with counsel before changing customer guidance.

- **Florida HB 657 signed, effective July 1, 2026 — major HOA governance overhaul**
  Source: <https://www.flcaa.com/post/legislation-impacting-florida-homeowners-associations>
  What happened: HB 657 (passed FL House 108-2 in March 2026) introduces new HOA termination procedures, expanded electronic balloting, and enhanced disclosure requirements; mandatory website/portal transparency for associations with 100+ parcels also kicks in.
  So what for Brand 9: New transparency mandates create demand for physical community identification and directional signage as HOAs formalize their governance presence — target community managers mid-year as they stand up new websites and infrastructure.

- **EGD trend: biophilic wayfinding + sustainable materials displacing conventional monument specs**
  Source: <https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/>
  What happened: SEGD and industry publishers confirm that master-planned community developers and commercial clients are now specifying reclaimed wood, recycled metals, and low-VOC inks for monument and wayfinding packages; biophilic design principles are reducing literal sign count while raising per-unit value.
  So what for Brand 9: Upgrading material offerings to include one or two "sustainable monument" SKUs positions Brand 9 ahead of spec changes that Florida's lagoon and conservation-adjacent communities will increasingly require.

- **Source:** [NAHB — Acquisitions Increasing Among Home Builders](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/07/acquisitions-increasing-among-home-builders) (also: [LBM Journal](https://www.lbmjournal.com/industry-news/press-release/15830722/national-association-of-home-builders-acquisitions-increasing-among-home-builders), [MortgagePoint](https://themortgagepoint.com/2026/07/30/acquisitions-on-the-rise-among-home-builders/))
  **What happened:** NAHB reports M&A among builders rose sharply — share of builders seeing local M&A activity went from 14% (Aug 2025) to 21% (June 2026), and builders "approached for acquisition" doubled from 9% to 18%; recent deals cited include Taylor Morrison (Berkshire Hathaway), Tri Pointe Homes (Sumitomo Forestry), and United Homes Group (Stanley Martin).
  **So what for Brand 9:** Every acquired builder eventually needs a rebrand — monument signs, sales-center signage, and community entry markers get swapped when a parent brand takes over. This consolidation wave is a lead-gen signal: track acquired builders' active FL/SC/GA/TX communities for signage refresh RFPs over the next 6–12 months.

**4. EGD/Wayfinding 2026 trend: biophilic design + sustainability materials dominating specs**
- **Source:** [SEGD – Sustainability Insights for EGD](https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/)
- **What happened:** The leading 2026 EGD trend is biophilic wayfinding (green walls, water features as navigational cues, reducing literal signage density by ~40%) combined with client mandates for recycled acrylics, reclaimed wood, and low-VOC materials. - **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder amenity centers and master-planned communities are prime adopters of biophilic EGD. Brand 9 should develop a spec-sheet and sample portfolio around sustainable monument/wayfinding materials to lead with in new-community pitches.

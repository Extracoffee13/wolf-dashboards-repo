- **Dream Finders closed its $2.2B Beazer Homes buy (Oct 20). It is now about the #6 U.S. builder, and it is based in Jacksonville.**
  - Source: https://www.bdmag.com/ (rolling feed; check against DFH/Beazer press releases)
  - What happened: Beazer communities are going to the Dream Finders brand. Separately, Century Communities closed HistoryMaker Homes on Oct 7, adding 29 DFW communities (https://investors.centurycommunities.com/news/news-details/2026/Century-Communities-Triples-Dallas-Fort-Worth-Footprint-with-Acquisition-of-HistoryMaker-Homes/default.aspx).

- **Wayfinding fabrication and install RFPs keep coming in GA, SC and TX.**
  - Sources: https://www.highergov.com/sl/contract-opportunity/ga-rfp-26-24-fabrication-installation-o-60325755/ · https://caycesc.gov/newsfiles/wayfinding.pdf · https://govdirections.com/bids/sitemap/none/92/supplies-signs-scoreboards-wayfinding-signage-interior-exterior/
  - What happened: Henry County, GA has RFP 26-24 out for wayfinding fabrication and install, and the City of Cayce, SC has a citywide wayfinding RFP out. Neither showed a due date I could confirm. Most FL and TX sign bids closed Nov 4–11.
  - So what for Brand 9: check whether Henry County and Cayce are still open on their portals, then log both in B9_BIDS.json. Fab-and-install packages fit B9 better than design-only master plans.

- **Image360 made Entrepreneur's 2026 Top Franchises for Veterans (Nov 11), but its location count fell from 330 (2014) to 272.**
  - Sources: https://www.signshop.com/news/allegra-marketing-print-mail-and-image360-named-among-top-franchises-for-veterans/ · https://signsofthetimes.com/will-sign-companies-meet-2026-goals/
  - What happened: Alliance Franchise Brands is promoting Image360 and Allegra for franchise recruiting while the Image360 network shrinks.
  - So what for Brand 9: franchise sign shops are thinning out. Builder-specific specialization (decks, standards, fast refaces) is a stronger moat than general retail sign work.

- **Florida cities are rewriting their temporary sign rules (Venice took effect after Nov 3; Kissimmee adopted; Port Orange has a digital-sign ordinance pending).**
  - Sources: https://citizenportal.ai/articles/10008578/florida/sarasota-county/venice/council-adopts-temporary-sign-code-changes-to-take-effect-after-nov-3 · https://citizenportal.ai/articles/10251111/Florida/Osceola-County/Kissimmee/Commission-adopts-sign-code-update-changing-nonresidential-yard-sign-rules
  - What happened: the changes cover yard and temporary signs, mostly matching city rules to county rules after the election.
  - So what for Brand 9: builder directional and weekend signs fall under these temporary-sign rules. Keep a per-city note in the decks so B9 does not spec signs that get pulled. (Keep public copy free of permit claims, per the claims gate.)

## Permitting / Regulatory

- **Florida HOA reforms (HB 1203) fully in force 2026 — HOAs retain full sign-restriction authority**
  - Source: https://fixmyhoaviolation.com/states/florida | https://managecasa.com/articles/new-hoa-laws-in-florida
  - What happened: HB 1203 caps HOA fines at $100 with mandatory 14-day notice, but the law does not limit HOA authority to regulate or ban community signage. HOAs in FL master-planned communities can still prohibit all exterior signs except a single security sign within 10 ft of the front entry.
  - So what for Brand 9: Homebuilders installing community-wide sign programs in new HOA communities must design within CC&R restrictions from day one; Brand 9 should offer a "HOA-compliant sign package" review service as a sales differentiator.

## EGD / Industry Trend

- **EGD is shifting to XGD (experiential graphic design) — monument signs increasingly integrated with digital and sustainable materials**
  - Source: https://www.signsbytomorrow.com/arlingtonheights/environmental-signage/experiential-graphics | https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/
  - What happened: Leading sign designers are replacing static monument and wayfinding programs with XGD systems: LED insets, touch-based directories, recycled-metal + low-VOC finishes, and motion-activated elements. SEGD is actively publishing sustainability toolkits for sign specifiers.
  - So what for Brand 9: Homebuilder clients will increasingly request "sustainable monument" and digitally enhanced entry experiences for premium communities; Brand 9 should develop a materials/spec sheet for eco-forward monument packages to pitch to Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, and Meritage on higher-end communities.

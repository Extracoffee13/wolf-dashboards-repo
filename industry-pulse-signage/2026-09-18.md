  **What happened:** Dream Finders Homes' $2.2B acquisition of Beazer Homes cleared a bondholder consent solicitation (dated Sept 9, 2026) and remains on track to close Q4 2026, creating the 6th-largest U.S. homebuilder (~13,000 annual closings).

- **Source:** https://www.census.gov/construction/nrc/pdf/newresconst.pdf

## Sign-Industry Competitors

- **Source:** https://whattheythink.com/news/131124-fastsigns-reports-record-breaking-first-half-2026-franchise-development-momentum-continues/
  **What happened:** FASTSIGNS reported a record H1 2026 for franchise development — 13 new centers opened, 18 new franchise agreements signed (incl. Australia, Canada, UK, Dominican Republic) — and is ranked #1 in its category on Entrepreneur's Franchise 500 for the 10th straight year.

## Signage / EGD Trends

- **Source:** https://www.thesignpack.com/signage-industry-trends/
  **What happened:** 2026 trend coverage confirms wayfinding/EGD is now being designed as one integrated system with monument signage from the start (not bolted on later), with halo-lit, low-profile monument designs favored over older backlit box signs.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Opportunity to upsell homebuilder clients on bundled monument + wayfinding + EGD packages framed as "community identity systems" rather than one-off entry signs — higher ticket, stickier relationship.

**5. EGD/Wayfinding: Biophilic Design + Sustainability Mandates Reshaping Specs**
- **Source:** [SEGD](https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/) | [Identity Group](https://www.identitygroup.com/environmental-graphic-design-trends-used-in-signage/)
- **What happened:** 2026 EGD trend reports highlight biophilic wayfinding (green walls, natural light paths reducing literal sign count by ~40%) and updated accessibility standards (BS 8300-2 late 2025 mandating tactile/acoustic wayfinding). Sustainable materials — reclaimed wood, recycled metals, low-VOC inks — are now spec'd by default on institutional projects.
- **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder community entry packages and HOA monument signs are shifting toward natural-material finishes and integrated lighting; Brand 9 should have eco-material and ADA/accessible wayfinding options in its standard quote library.

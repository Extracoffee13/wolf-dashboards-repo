# Industry Pulse — Signage & Homebuilders — 2026-08-18

- **Berkshire Hathaway closed its acquisition of Taylor Morrison** (~$6.8B equity / ~$8.5B enterprise value, closed July 24, 2026); Taylor Morrison keeps CEO Sheryl Palmer and folds into Berkshire's site-built homebuilding operations. [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562476/000119312526316037/d148123dex991.htm)
  *So what:* A top-10 builder just changed ownership — expect procurement/branding decisions for Taylor Morrison communities to run through new corporate channels for a while. Track for disruption or opportunity in existing FL community signage relationships.

- **D.R. Horton overtook Lennar as the #1-volume national homebuilder**, pulling 3,946 permits in Q1 2026 alone. [Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/new-homes/d-r-horton-overtook-lennar-as-no-1-homebuilder-3q-3535433/)
  *So what:* Builder market-share is reshuffling at the top — worth re-weighting B9's builder-relationship outreach priority list toward D.R. Horton's FL/SC/GA/TX active communities.

- **FASTSIGNS reports record-breaking H1 2026 franchise development**, continuing its #1 Franchise 500 signage-category ranking for a 10th consecutive year. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fastsigns-reports-record-breaking-first-half-of-2026-for-franchise-development-as-momentum-continues-302834648.html)
  *So what:* National competitor is expanding footprint aggressively — B9 should sanity-check whether new FASTSIGNS locations are opening near FL/SC/GA/TX target metros and adjust local positioning/pricing accordingly.

- **Florida HB 657 (HOA reform)** passed the House 108-2 (March 5, 2026) and remains active — would let owners dissolve HOAs by supermajority, create a dedicated Community Associations Court, and fine boards up to $5,000/violation for withholding records. [fixmyhoaviolation.com](https://fixmyhoaviolation.com/states/florida)
  *So what:* If enacted, HOA governance churn could shake loose stalled monument/entry-sign approval processes (both risk and opportunity) — worth flagging to any B9 reps working HOA-gated communities.

- **2026 signage trend reports converge on: monument signs favoring clean/legible layouts in routed aluminum and eco-conscious substrates, and wayfinding/EGD being designed into the environment rather than bolted on afterward.** [Identity Group](https://www.identitygroup.com/environmental-graphic-design-trends-used-in-signage/), [Sign-Age](https://www.sign-age.com/blogs/news/signage-design-trends-for-2026)
  *So what:* Reinforces B9's pitch angle for master-planned community EGD packages — lead with integrated wayfinding + sustainable materials rather than standalone monument signs.

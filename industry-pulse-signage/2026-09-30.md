# Industry Pulse: Signage + Homebuilders, 2026-09-30

Coverage note: web search returned no strictly last-24h items. Bullets use the freshest available (September 2026). No FL/SC/GA/TX government signage RFPs and no Florida sign-code changes surfaced for the target cities.

- **Lennar margin squeeze and incentives**
  - Source: https://dataporium.tech/articles/homebuilder-margins-and-incentives-in-2026-what-lennar-horton-and-pulte-results-show
  - What happened: Lennar's Q3 gross margin was 15.8% with incentives around 12% of price, and new orders were down 9% as of Sep 16. D.R. Horton's cancellation rate rose to 20%. The 30-year mortgage rate was 6.95% on Sep 17.
  - So what: Builders are cutting cost and pacing launches. Expect price pressure on community signage packages and slower, more phased sign orders. Lead with value and speed, not premium upsell.

- **D.R. Horton is now the #1 builder and Toll Brothers is buying**
  - Source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/new-homes/d-r-horton-overtook-lennar-as-no-1-homebuilder-3q-3535433/ and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000794170/000079417026000102/tol-20260731.htm
  - What happened: D.R. Horton overtook Lennar as the largest builder by volume. Toll Brothers bought Buffington Homes (AR, 9 communities, ~1,500 lots) in May 2026.
  - So what: D.R. Horton is a large, under-pursued account beyond Lennar. Acquired communities also need re-branding signage.

- **Franchise competitors are expanding**
  - Source: https://whattheythink.com/news/131124-fastsigns-reports-record-breaking-first-half-2026-franchise-development-momentum-continues/ and https://www.franchising.com/news/20260921_two_hagerstown_print_and_signage_businesses_have_rebranded_now_operating_as.html
  - What happened: FASTSIGNS opened 13 centers and signed 18 agreements in H1 2026. Allegra/Image360 keep absorbing independent print and sign shops (Hagerstown rebrand, Sep 2026).
  - So what: Franchise competition is growing in the small-format market. Brand 9's edge is custom monument, wayfinding and builder-program work that franchises don't handle well.

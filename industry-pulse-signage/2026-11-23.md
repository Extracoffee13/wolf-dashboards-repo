- **Dream Finders Homes closed its Beazer Homes acquisition (about $2.2B enterprise value, closed Oct 20, 2026).**
  Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001825088/000162828026066548/ex991closingpr.htm
  So what for Brand 9: Dream Finders is based in Jacksonville. Beazer's communities may be rebranded, which would mean new monument faces, model park signs, flags and sales center interiors. Pitch Dream Finders' JAX HQ on a rebrand rollout before a national vendor gets the work.

- **Toll Brothers announced Sagebrush at EverRange, a new golf cart-friendly master-planned community in Jacksonville (Nov 11, 2026). It is listed as coming soon.**
  Source: https://finviz.com/news/421086/toll-brothers-announces-new-luxury-homes-coming-soon-to-jacksonville-florida
  So what for Brand 9: Coming soon is the stage where entry monuments, frontage 4x8s and wayfinding get bought. It is also in the same EverRange master plan as Toll's Mariposa and Regency. Golf cart routes add custom wayfinding and cart-path signs to the scope.

- **Toll Brothers opened the Egret Collection at Monterey, Lakewood Ranch, for sale, with its sales center and models at 2637 Waterfront Circle (Nov 16, 2026).**
  Source: https://finviz.com/news/423604/toll-brothers-announces-new-collection-now-open-for-sale-at-monterey-at-lakewood-ranch-in-florida
  So what for Brand 9: Toll keeps launching new collections inside existing Florida communities. Each one needs its own collection ID signs, model IDs and lot signs, which suits a repeat program-pricing pitch.

- **Century Complete released Belle Lago in Lake Wales in mid-November 2026: entry-level homes from the low $300s, no CDD fees.**
  Source: https://finviz.com/news/408963/century-complete-announces-november-opening-for-belle-lago-offering-new-homes-in-lake-wales-fl-with-select-lakeview-homesites-and-no-cdd-fees
  So what for Brand 9: Entry-level builders in Polk County compete on price and buy low-cost frontage signage. Polk also passed a $250-per-sign right-of-way fine in March 2026, so selling compliant sign placement is a real advantage there.

- **Clay County (JAX metro) is moving a sign-code amendment that creates an optional master sign plan for very large developments (Planning Commission recommended it Apr 7, 2026; the Board's vote is unconfirmed).**
  Source: https://citizenportal.ai/articles/7944213/Florida/Clay-County/Commission-backs-countywide-master-sign-plan-rules-for-very-large-developments
  So what for Brand 9: Master sign plans let large Clay County communities run whole coordinated sign packages. Brand 9 should know the rule so it can quote and design to it. Check the Board's agenda for adoption.

- **Source:** [Environmental Graphics: The 2026 Strategic Guide to Branded Spaces — Linemark](https://www.linemark.com/environmental-graphics-the-2026-strategic-guide-to-branded-spaces/) / [4 Signage Industry Trends to Watch in 2026 — FASTSIGNS](https://www.fastsigns.com/blog/about-products/digital-signage/4-signage-industry-trends-to-watch-in-2026/)
  **What happened:** 2026 EGD/monument trends are converging on modular sign systems (swap panels/lighting without full replacement) and mandatory sustainable materials (reclaimed wood, recycled metal, low-VOC inks).
  **So what for Brand 9:** Modular monument offerings give homebuilder clients a lower-cost refresh path between full community rebrands — worth adding as a line item in builder proposals.

- **Source:** [The 2025 Florida Statutes, Ch. 2026-64 (CS/CS/CS/HB 927) — land planning & development](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499%2F0479%2F0479.html)
  **What happened:** New FL legislation (Ch. 2026-64) touches local land-planning and development review processes statewide; no standalone sign-code overhaul was found this cycle, but permitting/development timelines are shifting under the new law.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Flag this for our permit-runner workflow — county sign-permit turnaround times may shift as jurisdictions implement the new development-review rules; monitor county bulletins in Jacksonville/Tampa/Orlando.

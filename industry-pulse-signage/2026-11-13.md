# Industry Pulse — Signage + Homebuilders — 2026-11-13

_Scan window: last 24h (to 2026-11-13). Signal was thin this cycle. Only the market item (#1) is from the last 24h; the rest are open opportunities or context still in play. I found no new M&A or community launches from Lennar, DR Horton, KB, Pulte, Toll, Meritage, Ashton Woods, MI Homes, Beazer or NVR, and no competitor news (FastSigns, Image360, Identiti, Stratus, Speedpro, Allegra) in the window._

- **Builders are looking for a bottom.** New-home inventory is at 8.4 months, and the NAHB/Wells Fargo HMI fell to 29, its lowest since June 2012. Existing-home sales hit a 16-year low in October.
  - Source: https://www.housingwire.com/articles/resale-rental-competition-builders/ · https://eyeonhousing.org/
  - **So what for Brand 9:** Builders will open fewer communities and spend more on incentives to move standing inventory. Pitch fast, cheap promo signage ("$X toward closing costs", quick-move-in riders, feather flags, avenue banners, A-frame refaces) to Lennar JAX/GC rather than waiting on new monument packages. Keep pricing tight, because marketing budgets are under margin pressure.

- **Texas annual contract for facility ID and wayfinding signage was posted Oct 29.** A separate "Exterior Wayfinding Signage" TX solicitation closed Nov 4.
  - Source: https://www.highergov.com/sl/contract-opportunity/tx-wayfinding-signage-61430760/
  - **So what for Brand 9:** Annual on-call signage contracts are the steadiest government work. Log it in B9_BIDS.json and run the go/no-go: SAM/portal registration, TX licensing partner and bonding are the gates. Houston "Outside Printing Services and Signage" (27R-011LM, due 2027-02-03) is a second, slower-deadline TX target: https://publicbidtracker.com/texas/open-bids/

- **Florida coastal sign restrictions are being litigated.** Property owners sued in M.D. Fla. over a city's emergency, then permanent, sign-code ban on private beachfront signs. Polk County's right-of-way sign-enforcement ordinance (March 2026) is also live.
  - Source: https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.flmd.462587/gov.uscourts.flmd.462587.1.0.pdf · https://www.polkfl.gov/news/polk-county-commissioners-approve-new-polk-county-right-of-way-sign-enforcement-ordinance-march-18-2026/
  - **So what for Brand 9:** Florida counties are tightening enforcement on temporary and ROW signs, the same bandit and directional signs builders lean on in a slow market. Steer builder clients toward on-site and private-lot placements and check local code before installs. Per the claims gate, keep permit talk out of public copy.

- **Homebuilder consolidation is still reshaping buyers.** Berkshire closed Taylor Morrison and is folding it in with Clayton's site-built builders under TM management. Sumitomo Forestry closed Tri Pointe in May.
  - Source: https://www.probuilder.com/home/article/55381025/berkshire-hathaway-to-acquire-taylor-morrison · https://www.zelmanassociates.com/resources/zelman-insights/2026-06/homebuilding-m-a-enters-a-new-phase-as-private-con
  - **So what for Brand 9:** Integration usually brings brand-standard refreshes and vendor consolidation. Taylor Morrison and Clayton communities in JAX and coastal GA (Esplanade at St. Marys) are worth a targeted intro before national sign-vendor contracts lock.

**5. EGD trend: biophilic wayfinding reducing monument signage count by ~40%**
- **Source:** [Environmental Graphics and Wayfinding Integration — Blink Signs](https://blinksigns.com/environmental-graphics-wayfinding-integration/)
- **What happened:** 2026 EGD design firms are deploying biophilic wayfinding (green walls, water features as directional cues) that reduces literal sign count by an estimated 40%, while demand for integrated digital wayfinding (haptic, light-pathing, touch directories) grows. Eco-material spec (reclaimed wood, recycled metal, low-VOC inks) is now standard in RFPs.
- **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder community launches increasingly want EGD packages, not just monument signs. Brand 9 should develop a bundled "community identity system" offering (monument + wayfinding + EGD consultation) to avoid being commoditized to the low-sign-count model that biophilic design enables.

- **FDOT 2026 Traffic Engineering Manual (TEM) Chapter 2 — Signs now in effect**
  Source: https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/traffic/trafficservices/studies/tem/tem-2026/2026-tem---chapter-2---signs.pdf
  What happened: FDOT published updated technical standards for highway signage as part of its 2026 TEM, governing materials, placement, and visibility specs for any signs near state road right-of-way.
  So what for Brand 9: Community entrance and monument signs near FDOT corridors must comply with these updated specs — Brand 9 can offer a compliance review service as a value-add when quoting FL homebuilder projects, reducing builder permit risk.

- **EGD/Wayfinding 2026 trend: biophilic design + sustainable substrates reducing sign count by ~40%**
  Source: https://blinksigns.com/environmental-graphics-wayfinding-integration/
  What happened: SEGD and industry designers are pushing biophilic wayfinding (green walls, water features as navigation cues) and eco-materials (recycled acrylics, reclaimed wood, low-VOC) as the leading 2026 EGD trend.
  So what for Brand 9: Master-planned community developers increasingly request sustainable wayfinding packages — positioning Brand 9's community signage programs around EGD + sustainability storytelling differentiates from commodity vendors and supports HOA/community branding narratives.

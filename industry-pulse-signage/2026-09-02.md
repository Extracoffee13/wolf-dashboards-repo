# Industry Pulse — Signage + Homebuilders — 2026-09-02

- **Source:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fastsigns-reports-record-breaking-first-half-of-2026-for-franchise-development-as-momentum-continues-302834648.html
  **What happened:** FASTSIGNS reports record-breaking H1 2026 franchise development (13 new centers opened, 18 agreements signed) and is aggressively expanding its NYC footprint with co-branded locations.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Direct national competitor is scaling franchise density fast — watch for a FASTSIGNS unit opening or co-branding into FL/GA/TX metros Brand 9 serves; worth a local competitive scan in Jacksonville/Tampa/Atlanta/Austin.

- **Source:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/new-homes/d-r-horton-overtook-lennar-as-no-1-homebuilder-3q-3535433/
  **What happened:** D.R. Horton overtook Lennar as the #1 U.S. homebuilder by Q3 volume.
  **So what for Brand 9:** D.R. Horton is the builder to prioritize for community-entrance and monument-signage outreach right now — its community counts (including active GA/FL projects like Altama in Brunswick, GA and Rice Hope near Savannah) are growing faster than Lennar's.

- **Source:** https://resourcecenter.lennar.com/our-communities/georgia/lennar-is-now-building-in-peach-and-houston-counties-ga ; https://finviz.com/news/100594/lennar-celebrates-the-grand-opening-of-seaton-creek-amenity-center
  **What happened:** Lennar opened new communities in Peach/Houston Counties, GA, and celebrated the grand-opening of the Seaton Creek amenity center in a Jacksonville, FL master-planned community.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Jacksonville is a core Brand 9 metro — a live Lennar grand-opening there is an immediate lead for entrance monuments, amenity-center signage, and wayfinding packages; worth a direct outreach call this week.

- **Source:** https://www.fastsigns.com/blog/about-products/digital-signage/4-signage-industry-trends-to-watch-in-2026/ ; https://www.identitygroup.com/environmental-graphic-design-trends-used-in-signage/
  **What happened:** 2026 signage/EGD trend coverage converges on two points: wayfinding is now designed as a fully-integrated part of the environment (not bolted on), and sustainable materials (recycled aluminum composite, FSC timber, water-based eco-solvent inks) are now a baseline client expectation, not a premium upsell.
  **So what for Brand 9: **Bake "sustainable substrate" options and integrated-wayfinding language into proposals by default — builders and property managers are starting to expect it unprompted, and leading with it is a differentiator against smaller sign shops still selling static monument signs.

- **Source:** https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/657/Analyses/h0657a.HAT.PDF
  **What happened:** Florida HB 657 (HOA reform: supermajority dissolution, a dedicated Community Associations Court, penalties for board record-withholding) passed the House 108-2 but died in the Senate when the 2026 session ended without a companion bill.
  **So what for Brand 9:** No near-term change to Florida's presuit-mediation requirement for HOA covenant/sign disputes — existing Chapter 720 rules (and the friction they create around HOA monument/entry-sign approvals) hold through 2026; revisit if HB 657 is refiled next session.

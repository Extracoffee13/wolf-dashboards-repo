- **Source:** https://www.fdot.gov/traffic/trafficservices/trafficstudies.shtm/traffic-engineering-manual
- **What happened:** FDOT released the 2026 Traffic Engineering Manual Chapter 2 (Signs), updating standards for distance signage on non-limited-access highways, bicycle sign placement, rest-area plaques, and CRIME WATCH sign installation on the State Highway System.
- **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder community monument and wayfinding signs near state roads must comply with 2026 TEM. Proactively audit client sites adjacent to FDOT ROW and offer a compliance + permit-pull service as a billable add-on.

**5. EGD Trend — Biophilic Wayfinding + Eco-Materials Are Now Entering Specs**
- **Source:** https://blinksigns.com/environmental-graphics-wayfinding-integration/
- **What happened:** Leading EGD firms are embedding biophilic design cues (green walls, water features as wayfinding anchors) to reduce literal signage load by ~40%; high-VOC vinyl and non-recyclable acrylics are increasingly flagged as spec liabilities.
- **So what for Brand 9:** Resort-lifestyle homebuilder clients (Esplanade/Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, Meritage) are moving toward low-VOC inks and recycled substrates. Document Brand 9's eco-material capabilities and surface them explicitly in proposal language.

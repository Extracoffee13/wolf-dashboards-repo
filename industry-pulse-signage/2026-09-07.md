# Industry Pulse — Signage & Homebuilders — 2026-09-07

- **D.R. Horton retakes #1 homebuilder spot from Lennar in Q3, beats earnings** — D.R. Horton posted Q3 FY2026 EPS of $3.20 (beating consensus by 7%) on $9.23B revenue and overtook Lennar as the nation's largest homebuilder by closings, though it trimmed full-year FY2026 revenue guidance to $32.5-33B from $33.5-34.5B. [Source](https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/new-homes/d-r-horton-overtook-lennar-as-no-1-homebuilder-3q-3535433/)
  *So what for Brand 9*: A reshuffled builder pecking order plus trimmed guidance signals softer new-community starts ahead — prioritize outreach to D.R. Horton's FL/GA/TX division offices now, since their community count (and signage spend) is set to keep growing even as guidance cools.

- **Lennar opens three new townhome communities in Southwest Florida** — Lennar announced three fresh townhome communities in SW Florida, sending shares up 2.24% on the news. [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LEN/news/)
  *So what for Brand 9*: Each new community needs entry monuments, directional/wayfinding, and model-home signage — SW Florida (Naples/Fort Myers corridor) is a live near-term lead-gen target for monument and EGD packages.

- **Century Communities keeps up an aggressive grand-opening cadence across GA, FL, and TX** — Multiple new communities and model-home openings announced in Fairburn/Dallas/Austell/McDonough GA, Alford/Fleming Island FL, and Forney TX. [Source](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CCS/century-communities-atlanta-continues-grand-opening-streak-with-new-q2gd4yq26oc6.html)
  *So what for Brand 9*: Century is one of the most active builders opening new sites across our exact FL/GA/TX target footprint right now — a strong candidate for a recurring "new community signage package" pitch rather than one-off deals.

- **PulteGroup and Toll Brothers post resilient but margin-compressed builder earnings** — Pulte beat Q2 estimates but gross margin contracted 200bps YoY to 25%; Toll Brothers beat with $2.97 EPS and $2.65B in home sales revenue. [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/toll-brothers-report-q3-earnings-145000460.html)
  *So what for Brand 9*: Margin pressure across builders means signage vendors should lead with value/turnaround-time messaging (not premium pricing) when pitching production packages this cycle.

- **2026 signage trend reports converge on "clean, legible monument signs" and sustainable EGD materials** — Multiple industry trend pieces highlight monument signage moving toward roadside visibility balanced with minimalist design, plus reclaimed wood/recycled metal materials and digital wayfinding integration. [Source](https://www.sign-age.com/blogs/news/signage-design-trends-for-2026)
  *So what for Brand 9*: Refresh Brand 9's monument-sign portfolio/marketing to emphasize legibility-first design and sustainable materials — this is becoming the default ask from builder marketing teams, not a differentiator.

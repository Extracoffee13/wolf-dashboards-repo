2. **Collier County (Naples, FL) opens a "County Signage" contractor pool.** It covers design, fabrication and installation of interior and exterior signage, with work awarded through quotes. The listed bid date is **2026-11-20**.
   - Source: https://www.constructionbidsource.com/state/florida/county/collier?page=1
   - So what for Brand 9: A pool contract is a low-risk way to get onto a government vendor list in FL. The deadline is 8 days out. Run it through the b9-bid-engine go/no-go: the scope includes foundations, electrical and permits, so check the licensing-partner gate first.

3. **Dream Finders Homes closed its ~$2.2B all-cash acquisition of Beazer Homes (October),** making it the #6 US builder. Century Communities also closed HistoryMaker Homes (Dallas, 29 communities).
   - Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001825088/000162828026066548/ex991closingpr.htm
   - So what for Brand 9: Dream Finders is headquartered in Jacksonville. When Beazer communities are rebranded into DFH, every monument, model ID and frontage sign has to be redone. That rebrand work is the opportunity, and the buyer is local.

4. **Lennar files plans for a 546-home community near Sunbridge (Osceola/Orlando),** next to Pulte and Toll's jointly built Woodline (436 homes). The same week, Lennar, Pulte and Meritage gave away Veterans Day homes. Lennar incentives sit around 12% with orders down 9% YoY.
   - Source: https://www.growthspotter.com/2026/10/14/lennar-to-join-homebuilder-lineup-in-sunbridge/
   - So what for Brand 9: Builders keep opening new communities even with soft orders and high incentives. That means steady demand for frontage and model-park signage, but tight budgets, so lead with standard deck pricing over custom upsells. Sunbridge has three builders on adjacent sites, which makes it a good Orlando foothold.

In design, the 2026 trend is "signage as a system": monument, entry directory and confirmation signs planned together, with clean, high-readability faces.
   - Source: https://sarasotasignshop.com/top-signage-trends-for-2026-sarasota-businesses-need-to-know
   - So what for Brand 9: Franchises keep adding South FL locations. Brand 9's edge with builders is selling a full community sign package (monument through wayfinding, one look) in place of one-off sign pieces.

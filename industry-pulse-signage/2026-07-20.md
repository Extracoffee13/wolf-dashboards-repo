# Industry Pulse — Signage & Homebuilders — 2026-07-20

- **NAHB builder confidence fell to 34 in July**, the 15th straight month below 40 (longest stretch since 2012); 37% of builders cut prices and 63% now use incentives. [ABA Banking Journal](http://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/07/home-builder-confidence-edged-down-to-34-in-july/)
  *So what:* Builders lean harder on sales-center traffic and promo signage to fight soft demand — near-term opportunity is incentive banners, directional/temporary signage refreshes, not new monument work.

- **21st Century ROAD to Housing Act became law July 11, 2026** (via automatic enactment after Trump declined to sign) — streamlines NEPA review, expands categorical exclusions for housing projects, and funds zoning/permitting modernization grants. [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/economy/new-housing-affordability-law-heres-what-it-means) / [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/6644)
  *So what:* Faster entitlement cycles nationally (incl. FL/SC/GA/TX) should widen the new-community pipeline over the next 12–18 months — worth tracking for a rebound in monument/wayfinding/sales-office signage RFPs from Lennar, DR Horton, Pulte, etc.

- **Florida HB 803 (permitting reform) + new "No Commercial Solicitation" sign law both took effect July 1, 2026** — HB 803 reduces permitting burden on smaller commercial work and clarifies permit-expiration windows; the solicitation law requires a standardized posted sign (min. 8.5"x11", 1" letters, statutory language) for legal effect. [GetYourLegalAction](https://www.getyourlegalaction.com/new-florida-laws-july-1-2026/) / [FloridaCourtRules](https://floridacourtrules.com/news/florida-no-commercial-solicitation-sign-law-501-062/)
  *So what:* HB 803 could shorten FL sign-permit turnaround on qualifying jobs; the solicitation-sign law is a small but concrete new SKU (compliant HOA/property signage) worth a quick product push in FL markets.

- **Sign-franchise consolidation continues**: Allegra Carol Stream acquired Image360 Lombard, and Image360 South Bay–Manhattan Beach merged with Custom Quick Sign, both bundling signage into broader print/marketing shops. [WhatTheyThink](https://whattheythink.com/news/129011-allegra-carol-stream-expands-capabilities-acquisition-image360-lombard/)
  *So what:* Franchise roll-ups are bundling signage with print/marketing services elsewhere in the country — a competitive pattern worth watching if it reaches Jacksonville/Tampa/Atlanta/Charleston; also a reminder that "signage + EGD as one package" is what buyers are being sold.

- **2026 EGD trend consensus: wayfinding is now designed as one system with environmental graphics from the start**, not bolted on after — bold minimalist monument signage, high-contrast type, sustainable materials (reclaimed wood, recycled metal). [Sign-Age](https://www.sign-age.com/blogs/news/signage-design-trends-for-2026)
  *So what:* Supports pitching homebuilder sales-center and community-entry work as an integrated EGD package rather than a standalone monument sign — higher ticket, matches what national trend pieces say clients now expect.

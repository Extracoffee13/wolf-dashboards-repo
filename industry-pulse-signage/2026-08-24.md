  **What happened:** NAHB Housing Market Index ticked up to 35 in August (still below 50, 16th straight month under 40); 35% of builders are cutting prices and 63% are using non-price incentives.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Builder demand is soft but not collapsing — expect continued pressure on builders to differentiate communities visually (banners, incentive signage, directional/wayfinding) to drive traffic, which favors low-cost, fast-turn signage packages over big capital monument builds right now.

  **So what for Brand 9:** A national builder is actively opening new Jacksonville-area communities despite a soft print — new-community signage packages (entry monuments, directional, model-home banners) are a live opportunity in B9's home Jacksonville market right now.

- **Source:** https://www.franchising.com/amp/news/20260819_allegra_lisle_expands_its_reach_with_the_acquisition_of_a_local_print_shop.html
  **What happened:** Allegra (parent of the Allegra/Image360 signage network) acquired a local print shop in Lisle, IL on 8/19, continuing its roll-up strategy of bolting print shops onto its signage/graphics franchise footprint.
  **So what for Brand 9:** National sign franchise networks (Allegra/Image360) are consolidating local competitors via M&A to bundle print+signage — worth watching whether that roll-up pattern reaches FL/SC/GA/TX independent shops B9 competes against locally.

- **Source:** https://www.journal-topics.com/articles/new-gateway-wayfinding-signs-going-up-across-glenview-this-week/
  **What happened:** Glenview, IL is installing a $395K municipal gateway/wayfinding package — 10 village gateway signs, 4 downtown monument signs, and 10 wayfinding signs — as a model for branded municipal signage programs.
  **So what for Brand 9:** This is a template B9 can pitch to FL/GA/SC municipalities and counties: a bundled gateway + monument + wayfinding RFP is a proven, fundable project type local governments are already running elsewhere.

- **Source:** https://www.govcb.com/statebrowse/state.bids-florida-City%20of%20Jacksonville
  **What happened:** City of Jacksonville has an active "Arlington Gateway Monument Sign" construction bid (SR 115/Arlington Expressway) listed among its open government solicitations.
  **So what for Brand 9:** A live monument-sign bid in B9's own metro is worth tracking directly on DemandStar/GovCB — confirm bid status and deadline this week and evaluate for a response.

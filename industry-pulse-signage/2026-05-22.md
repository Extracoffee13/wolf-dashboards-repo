- **Source:** [SEGD — Sustainability Insights for EGD, Signage & Wayfinding](https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/)
  **What happened:** The 2026 EGD market is converging on sustainability specs (recycled acrylics, reclaimed wood, low-VOC inks) and biophilic wayfinding — natural-element cues that reduce literal sign counts by up to 40%; accessibility mandates (BS 8300-2 updated late 2025) are adding tactile and acoustic wayfinding requirements to commercial specs.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder amenity centers and new-community entrances are prime targets for biophilic monument packages — lead proposals with eco-material callouts and ADA/tactile compliance language to differentiate from commodity sign shops.

- **Source:** [How to Get a Sign Permit in Florida — 2026 Guide (SignsAndLEDs)](https://signsandleds.com/how-to-get-a-sign-permit-in-florida/) | [FDOT Outdoor Advertising FAQ](https://www.fdot.gov/rightofway/OutdoorAdvertisingFAQ.shtm)
  **What happened:** Florida has no unified statewide commercial sign permit; each municipality enforces its own code, and FDOT controls highway right-of-way signage separately — no single 2026 state-level code change found, but permitting complexity remains a consistent friction point for homebuilder community entrance signs crossing county lines.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Packaging permit-pull service as a line item on homebuilder proposals (especially multi-county master-planned communities) is a margin-positive differentiator that large franchise shops rarely offer end-to-end.

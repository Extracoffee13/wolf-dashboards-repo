- **Source:** https://www.housingwire.com/articles/homebuilder-deals-sellers-pricing-2026/
  **What happened:** HousingWire (early Sept 2026) reports homebuilder M&A appetite is cooling — buyers are more selective and scrutinizing land strategy/operating performance, widening valuation gaps and leaving some would-be sellers on the market longer.

- **Source:** https://www.thesignpack.com/signage-industry-trends/ and https://www.fastsigns.com/blog/about-products/digital-signage/4-signage-industry-trends-to-watch-in-2026/

## EGD / Wayfinding Trend Watch
- **XGD is emerging:** Environmental Graphic Design is evolving into Experiential Graphic Design (XGD) — large LED walls, interactive murals, sound-responsive graphics. SEGD and industry leaders are formalizing the distinction.
- **Sustainability materials trending:** Reclaimed wood, recycled metals, biodegradable substrates, and low-VOC inks are becoming standard asks in commercial EGD RFPs.
- **Accessibility as standard:** High-contrast palettes, braille integration, and tactile lettering are showing up as baseline requirements in new public and mixed-use signage specs.

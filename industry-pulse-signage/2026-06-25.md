- **Source:** [EGD → XGD trend overview — Signs by Tomorrow](https://www.signsbytomorrow.com/arlingtonheights/environmental-signage/experiential-graphics) | [SEGD Sustainability Insights](https://segd.org/resources/sustainability-insights-environmental-graphic-design-signage-wayfinding-key-takeaways-resources/)
  **What happened:** The industry is shifting from static Environmental Graphic Design (EGD) to Experiential Graphic Design (XGD) — dynamic LED walls, interactive murals, sound-responsive installations — alongside a sustainability pivot to reclaimed wood, recycled metals, and low-VOC inks now expected by design firms.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Homebuilder amenity centers and community clubhouses are early adopters of XGD; offering an EGD-to-XGD upgrade path (static monument → illuminated or digital-integrated entry feature) differentiates from commodity sign shops and opens higher-ticket scopes.

# Signage + Homebuilder Industry Pulse — 2026-07-15

Note: true 24h-fresh, signage-specific news was thin today; the items below are the most recent, highest-signal developments found (most within the last several weeks) across homebuilder activity, federal housing policy, and sign-code/design trends.

- **Source:** https://www.housingwire.com/articles/berkshire-sells-d-r-horton-adds-lennar-analysis/ (13F filed May 15, 2026)
  **What happened:** Berkshire Hathaway continues rotating out of D.R. Horton and into Lennar (now ~10.1M LEN shares, ~$877M), betting on Lennar's land-optionality/systems-driven execution over the next 24–36 months of a soft, incentive-heavy housing market.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Lennar looks like the builder with the steadier community pipeline through this cycle — worth prioritizing for national-account monument/wayfinding outreach over DHI in the near term.

- **Source:** https://bipartisanpolicy.org/explainer/whats-in-the-21st-century-road-to-housing-act/ ; https://www.bakerbotts.com/thought-leadership/publications/2026/june/the-21st-century-road-to-housing-act-congress-passes-final-bill
  **What happened:** Congress passed the bipartisan 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act (Senate 85-5 June 22, House 358-32 June 23), the biggest federal housing-supply bill since 1990 — it streamlines permitting and environmental review to speed up new-community development.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Faster entitlement-to-groundbreaking timelines nationally means a coming wave of new master-planned communities needing monument, wayfinding, and sales-center signage — good leading indicator to ramp builder-relationship outreach now.

- **Source:** https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CCS/century-communities-atlanta-continues-grand-opening-streak-with-new-q2gd4yq26oc6.html ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/century-communities-announces-grand-opening-of-new-homes-at-echo-grove-in-cumming-ga-302811067.html
  **What happened:** Century Communities/Century Complete keeps opening new communities across the Southeast — two new Atlanta-metro communities plus Echo Grove in Cumming, GA (grand opening June 27) and Panama City, FL openings this spring.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Century is one of the most active volume builders in our GA/FL footprint right now — a strong near-term prospect for sales-center banners, directional signage, and community monument packages.

- **Source:** https://hoodline.com/2026/05/jacksonville-fast-track-zoning-gambit-puts-neighbors-on-the-clock/ ; https://www.jacksonvilletx.org/780/Sign-Permits
  **What happened:** Jacksonville, TX modernized its sign ordinance for the first time in nearly two decades (working with Planning Concepts/JEDCO), loosening restrictions on temporary and grand-opening signage; separately, Jacksonville, FL is fast-tracking zoning approvals with shorter notice windows.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Loosened temporary/grand-opening sign rules are a template worth watching for our FL/GA/TX metros — easier permitting for banners and A-frames around new-community launches translates directly into more billable installs.

- **Source:** https://www.sign-age.com/blogs/news/signage-design-trends-for-2026 ; https://blinksigns.com/environmental-graphics-wayfinding-integration/
  **What happened:** 2026 EGD/wayfinding trend consensus: fully-integrated wayfinding (not bolted-on), bold minimalist monument design, and sustainable materials (reclaimed wood, recycled metal, low-VOC inks) are replacing decorative/legacy monument styles.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Refresh our monument/wayfinding pitch deck to lead with integrated-system + sustainable-materials language — homebuilder marketing teams are already speaking this vocabulary in RFPs.

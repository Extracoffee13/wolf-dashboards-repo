## 5. Florida HOA Law Changes 2026 — HB 657 Eliminates Presuit Mediation Requirement
**Source:** [Dania Fernandez, Esq. — Jan 31, 2026](https://www.daniafernandez.com/2026/01/31/florida-condo-hoa-law-changes-every-board-needs-to-know-in-2026/)

**What happened:** HB 657 (passed FL House, March 2026) removes mandatory mediation before HOA litigation; 2026 also tightened transparency/records mandates under Chapters 718 & 720, exposing boards to faster legal action.

**So what for Brand 9:** HOA communities now face accelerated legal exposure for sign-code ambiguities and non-compliant wayfinding; Brand 9 can pitch compliance sign audits as a proactive service to FL HOA and master-planned community managers.

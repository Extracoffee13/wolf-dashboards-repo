- **Atlanta-metro grand openings: Century Communities' Sierra Brooke in Lawrenceville (Nov 14) and Toll's Kennemore models in Alpharetta (Nov 7)**
  - Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/century-communities-celebrates-grand-opening-of-sierra-brooke-offering-new-homes-in-lawrenceville-ga-from-the-400s-302936707.html · https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/03/3394252/1924/en/toll-brothers-announces-model-home-grand-opening-at-kennemore-in-alpharetta-georgia.html
  - What happened: Two builders opened Atlanta-area communities this month, one entry-level (from the $400s) and one luxury (from $1.15M).
  - So what for Brand 9: Builders are still launching Atlanta communities at both price points. Lennar's JAX/GC playbook (deck, A-frame, flags) is a ready proof to show Century and Toll's GA divisions.

- **Builder demand is soft: Tri Pointe Q3 net orders -20%, cancellations up to 16%; Meritage and Century carry high inventory relative to backlog**
  - Source: https://www.stocktitan.net/news/TPH/tri-pointe-homes-inc-reports-2026-third-quarter-yoglb9wgzt0t.html · https://www.housingwire.com/articles/builder-inventory-backlog-ratios/
  - What happened: Public builders report falling orders and growing spec inventory, and they are leaning on incentives.
  - So what for Brand 9: When spec homes pile up, builders need fast-turn incentive signage (rate-buydown riders, "move-in ready" flags, A-frames) more than new monuments. B9 should lead with quick, low-ticket refreshes and expect tighter budgets on capital signs.

- **Berkshire Hathaway consolidation: Taylor Morrison acquired (closed Jul 24) and being merged with Clayton Properties; Berkshire now holds ~11% of Lennar**
  - Source: https://www.resiclubanalytics.com/p/warren-buffett-successor-greg-abel-third-housing-market-bet-lennar-stock · https://www.fastcompany.com/91593204/housing-market-homebuilding-berkshire-hathaway-taylor-morrison-inside-details
  - What happened: Berkshire is now the #4 US builder and the largest outside holder of Lennar, B9's anchor client.
  - So what for Brand 9: The Taylor Morrison/Clayton merger is likely to bring brand-standard and vendor consolidation. If Taylor Morrison communities in FL/GA get rebranded, that means reface work, so watch for the merger's brand announcement.

- **Government RFPs: no open FL/SC/GA/TX signage bids found in the window; the most recent ones closed Nov 3–6 (Texas Southmost College exterior wayfinding, Eastern Wharf wayfinding in GA, a FL signage design/install bid)**

  - So what for Brand 9: Bid windows in this region are about 2–4 weeks, and aggregator coverage lags. B9 should track issuing-agency portals directly (b9-bid-engine) so it doesn't miss the next batch.

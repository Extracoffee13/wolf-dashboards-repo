- **Source:** https://jaredjones.com/2026/07/27/panther-run-clermont-master-planned-community/
  **What happened:** Panther Run, a 2,241-acre master-planned community in Clermont, FL (south Lake County), held a public open house Aug 10, 2026 for a plan covering roads, a proposed high school, and 2,600 homes.

  **So what for Brand 9:** A multifamily infill phase inside an established MPC is a smaller but faster-turnaround signage job (leasing/monument/building ID) — good near-term pipeline candidate given the 2028 delivery timeline gives lead time to pitch now.

- **Source:** https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/traffic/trafficservices/studies/tem/tem-2026/2026-tem---chapter-2---signs.pdf
  **What happened:** FDOT's 2026 Traffic Engineering Manual, Chapter 2 (Signs), and the companion 2026 FDOT Design Manual Topic 230 (Signing and Pavement Marking) are the current governing standards for FL roadway/regulatory signage as of this year.
  **So what for Brand 9:** Any FDOT right-of-way-adjacent monument or wayfinding work (common near new MPCs on state roads) should be checked against the current-year TEM/Design Manual before fabrication to avoid permitting rework.

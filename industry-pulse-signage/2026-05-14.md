## Macro Signal
Biophilic wayfinding design (using landscape and water features as navigation anchors, reducing literal signage density ~40%) is emerging as an EGD specification trend in 2026 master-planned communities. Brand 9 should be prepared to pitch integrated monument + landscape sign systems rather than standalone sign packages.

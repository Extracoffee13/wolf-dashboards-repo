## Observations

- **Anthropic is verticalizing fast.** The simultaneous launch of Claude for Legal, Claude for Small Business, and Claude for Creative Work signals a connector-first strategy — the skill layer is increasingly bundled by industry, not just by capability.
- **PyPI is the fastest-moving channel.** `tradingview-mcp-server`, `claude-agent-sdk`, and `skill-to-mcp` all shipped on PyPI before appearing in official directories. Monitoring PyPI weekly will catch finance/agent tooling earliest.
- **Open-source MCP servers are maturing.** `ms-365-mcp-server` now covers 200+ Graph API endpoints; parity with commercial connectors is close enough that self-hosted versions are viable for sensitive workloads.

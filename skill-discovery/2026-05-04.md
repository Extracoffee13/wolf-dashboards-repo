## Observations

- The MCP ecosystem continues rapid growth (~3,000+ listed servers as of May 2026); noise-to-signal ratio is high — most new entries are thin wrappers.
- Finance-specific MCP servers (Alpha Vantage, Financial Datasets, EODHD) are maturing quickly and offer verified data quality for capital-allocation workflows.
- Marketing measurement MCPs (Fospha) represent an emerging category where independent attribution data feeds directly into agentic budget optimization — a gap previously requiring human dashboard review.
- Skill-bridging tools (AgentSkills MCP, skill-to-mcp) are emerging as the infrastructure layer enabling skill portability across agent runtimes, which matters as The Construct scales beyond Claude-only deployments.

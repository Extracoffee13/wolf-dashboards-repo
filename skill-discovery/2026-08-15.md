### 1. Claude Cowork in the Chrome side panel — score 5
- Source: https://claude.com/blog/cowork-chrome-side-panel
- Pitch: The Chrome extension's side panel now runs a full Cowork session in-browser, with existing Skills and Connectors working with zero extra setup — turns every Chrome tab into a live agent workspace.
- Why it matters: The Construct already routes GBP edits, Higgsfield paid-account generation, and general browser work through Cowork/Claude-in-Chrome specifically because those flows need an authenticated human browser context. This closes the gap between "desktop Cowork" and "in-tab work" — same session, same history, no re-auth.
- Suggested owner: AP (session/browser-ops layer feeding Vector, WOLF, and GBP work)

- Source: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/ ; https://blog.cloudflare.com/mcp-v2/

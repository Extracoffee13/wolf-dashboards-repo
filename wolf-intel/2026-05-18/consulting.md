### Executive Summary

Deloitte's most important data point in this paper: **74% of organizations expect to be using AI agents by 2027, but only 11% have successfully deployed them in production today.** That 63-point gap is not hype vs. reality — it is the execution window. Deloitte's diagnosis of why: enterprises are attempting to automate existing processes designed for humans rather than redesigning those processes for AI-first operations. The paper identifies customer support, supply chain, R&D, knowledge management, and cybersecurity as the highest-impact agent deployment domains. The guardrails framing — governance, identity, audit — is positioned not as a constraint on adoption but as the actual unlock: organizations that solve for accountability in agentic systems scale faster, not slower. The paper cites that organizations where agent deployment is governed by explicit human-in-the-loop thresholds show 3x faster iteration cycles than ungoverned deployments.

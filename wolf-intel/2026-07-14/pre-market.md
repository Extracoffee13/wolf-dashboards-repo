# WOLF Pre-Market Intelligence — 2026-07-14

*Compiled overnight (US futures + Asia close + Europe open) ahead of the 09:30 ET open.*

## Headline

The Strait of Hormuz crisis is back and it's the whole tape today. US forces struck 80+ targets inside Iran over the July 12–13 weekend after the June 17 ceasefire collapsed; Iran's Revolutionary Guard retaliated by closing the Strait again and hit two UAE-flagged tankers (one crew death). Trump announced the US is reinstating an "Iranian blockade" — the Strait stays open to everyone except Iran — with a 20% toll on all cargo transiting, effective 4:00pm ET **today**. That's an oil shock landing in the same 24 hours as a soft CPI print and the first wave of bank earnings, which is why the tape is fighting itself: cooler inflation and beat-and-raise banks would normally be a rally, but energy-driven inflation risk and a live shooting war are capping it.

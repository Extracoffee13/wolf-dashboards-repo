## 1. The dominant story: Iran escalation is overriding the CPI rally

Overnight sentiment whipsawed. Tuesday night's setup was constructive — June CPI came in cooler than expected and ASML raised guidance, lifting the AI/chip trade globally. But the tape flipped after President Trump declared the Iran ceasefire "over," announced reinstatement of the naval blockade on Iranian vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz, and the US carried out a fresh round of strikes on Iran in the early hours of July 15. Iran's IRGC retaliated with strikes on US military sites in Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan. Trump has warned that "next week it gets really bad" — explicitly threatening Iranian power plants and bridges absent a negotiated breakthrough.

Because the news broke across the evening, headline futures levels vary by source/timestamp:
- Early-evening prints (pre-strike): S&P 500 futures +0.1–0.2%, Nasdaq-100 futures +0.4–0.5%, Dow futures flat, on cool-CPI/ASML optimism.
- Post-strike prints (NBC, overnight): Dow -0.8%, Nasdaq -0.2%, S&P -0.5%, as Hormuz/strike headlines hit risk appetite.
This is a genuine two-way, headline-driven session — treat any single futures snapshot as stale within the hour.

## FOMC / Fed — the real catalyst this week

- **FOMC decision: Wednesday, July 29**, with Chair Kevin Warsh's post-meeting press conference — his second policy decision since being confirmed (54-45, the closest modern-era vote) on May 13, 2026, replacing Jerome Powell.
- Warsh has publicly stated "prices are too high" and Trump has been vocal that he expects Warsh to cut rates, creating an unusual dynamic: a Trump-aligned chair inheriting a still-live inflation scare.
- Market pricing has been unusually split this cycle: as of late last week, CME-style tracking showed odds ranging ~61–65% hold vs. as much as ~34–38% probability of a **hike** (not a cut) at this meeting, driven by oil-price-fueled inflation fears from the now-paused Iran conflict.
- **This morning's oil crash is the key swing factor**: with WTI down ~7.7% and Brent under $90, the inflation-impulse argument for a hike should be fading fast. Watch whether hike-odds pricing unwinds through Tuesday — that's the single most important pre-FOMC tell.

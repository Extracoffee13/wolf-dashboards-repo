**1. Strait of Hormuz — Iran strikes Qatari LNG carrier.** IRGC missile hit the Al Rekayyat LNG carrier and damaged a Saudi crude tanker exiting Hormuz. Brent settled +3% to $74.16; WTI +2.8–2.9% to ~$70.41–70.44. The US revoked the general license authorizing Iranian oil sales in response, calling the strikes "wholly unacceptable." LNG cannot be rerouted around Hormuz the way crude can — this is a more structurally sticky risk premium than a typical crude-only scare. Watch European gas (reported +6% intraday) and any follow-on Gulf shipping/insurance headlines overnight.

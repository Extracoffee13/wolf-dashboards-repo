## Fed / Treasury / Macro Headlines

- **FOMC minutes (Jun 16–17 meeting, released Jul 8) were more hawkish than expected.** Under new Chair Kevin Warsh, the committee held rates at 3.50–3.75% but stripped out earlier rate-cut language. Committee is split: 9 members see at least one **hike** by year-end, 8 see no change, 1 sees a cut. Warsh submitted no dot at all — first Fed chair to abstain since the dot plot began in 2012.
- **CME FedWatch:** implied odds of a hike by September jumped to 68.8% (from 62% the day before); December hike odds at 85.3%.
- **This is a regime flip from "when does the Fed cut" to "will the Fed hike" — and equities are barely pricing it (VIX 15.93).** That's the gap to watch.
- **10Y Treasury yield:** 4.54%. **DXY:** 100.91.

## Iran / Strait of Hormuz — Geopolitical

- The US-Iran ceasefire signed last month broke down; the US struck Iranian coastal targets, and Iran retaliated by hitting tankers transiting the Strait of Hormuz (a Qatari LNG carrier, the *Al-Rekayyat*, and a second tanker reportedly hit by an unidentified projectile).
- The US revoked Iran's oil-sale license, which briefly spiked Brent to ~$76 intraday on July 7.
- Thursday (Jul 9): oil fell more than 2% on reports Iran is seeking negotiations — helping equities rally.
- **This morning (Jul 10):** Brent ~$76.80/bbl (down ~$2.45 vs. yesterday morning), WTI still under $72. Both remain elevated for the week despite Thursday's pullback — the Strait remains a live disruption risk (it handles ~20% of global oil traffic).
- Net: oil is not signaling all-clear, but equities are trading like it is. Watch XLE/energy complex as the tell if Hormuz headlines resume.

## Top Story: SK Hynix (SKHY) Nasdaq Debut

- Ticker **SKHY**, ADRs priced Thursday at $149, ~$26.5B raised (177.9M ADRs, each = 1/10 common share) — the largest-ever U.S. IPO/listing by a foreign company, surpassing Alibaba (2014) and Saudi Aramco, second only to SpaceX's record-breaking offering.
- Order book was 7x oversubscribed. Proceeds fund the Yongin Semiconductor Cluster fab, the P&T7 advanced packaging fab in Cheongju, and EUV lithography equipment purchases.
- SK Hynix's Seoul-listed shares have more than tripled YTD, making KOSPI the world's best-performing major index since the start of 2025.
- Pre-market chip names are mixed/lower — a classic "sell the setup, watch the debut" pattern after Thursday's 6–8% equipment-stock melt-up (LRCX, AMAT, KLAC, MU). Micron separately confirmed plans to expand U.S. chipmaking investment.
- **Watch:** where SKHY opens relative to the $149 print. A muted or negative open would be the first real crack in the AI-chip momentum trade this week.

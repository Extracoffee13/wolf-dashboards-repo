The **2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis** is still the dominant cross-asset driver. Iran has laid mines and attacked tankers intermittently since; a partial reopening (17M+ bbl transited Monday per US Energy Secretary Wright, the highest since the war began) is fighting against renewed flare-ups. This is a live tail-risk overhang for every session until it resolves — treat any Hormuz headline as capable of gapping futures 1%+ intraday.

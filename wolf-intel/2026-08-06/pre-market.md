## 7. Risk Headlines — FOMC / Treasury / Geopolitics

**Lead story: Strait of Hormuz de-escalation.** Oil has fallen sharply over the past three sessions on signs of a US/Iran interim deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz:
- Treasury Secretary **Scott Bessent** said on CNBC (Aug 4) that a deal "may come today or tomorrow."
- Iran reportedly reached agreement with **Oman** on a proposed shipping route through the waterway, and is reportedly considering allowing European nations to help clear mines.
- **Brent** ~$83.51/bbl (-5.09% on the session cited), **WTI** ~$79.87/bbl (-5.67%), extending a >10% two-session decline referenced earlier in the week.
- ~20-21% of global petroleum liquids flow through the Strait — this is the single largest macro risk-on catalyst in the market right now, and the size of the oil move reflects a large geopolitical-premium unwind, not a demand story.

**Rates/gold:** 10Y Treasury yield ~4.63% (as of Aug 5). Gold ~$4,156.51, +1.94% on the day cited — gold strength alongside falling oil and a de-escalation catalyst is a slightly inconsistent cross-asset signal worth flagging; could reflect residual macro/inflation hedging rather than pure geopolitical fear.

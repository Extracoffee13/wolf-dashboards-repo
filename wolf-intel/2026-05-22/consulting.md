**Executive Summary:**
McKinsey synthesizes the current state of agentic AI deployment across real estate operations, moving beyond the "isolated tool" framing that dominated 2024–2025 discourse. The paper introduces a "domain" model for AI transformation: rather than improving individual workflow steps, leading operators are rethinking leasing, operations, and asset management as end-to-end domains co-designed with human–agent partnerships. Quantified outcomes from early implementations include 10–30% improvements in net operating income (NOI), operating costs, and cycle times when agents are coordinated across a full workflow rather than solving one step. The paper argues that firms still in "pilot mode" are not approaching AI wrong — they are approaching it at the wrong scale. The strategic lever is domain redesign, not tool adoption.

### 2. PwC — "Data and AI Readiness for PE Portfolio Companies"

**Published:** ~May 19–20, 2026 (updated 3 days prior to scan date)
**URL:** https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/financial-services/private-equity/data-ai-pe-portcos.html
**Score: 4 / 5 — sharpens a thesis**

**Executive Summary:**
PwC's updated guide for middle-market PE portfolio companies diagnoses a persistent structural gap: most portcos lag large enterprises in AI and data maturity due to chronic underinvestment in core operating infrastructure. The symptoms are familiar — fragmented systems, limited automation, inconsistent data, reliance on manual workarounds and tribal knowledge. PwC frames this not as a failure of ambition but as a failure of sequencing, and offers three strategic principles: (1) favor reliability over novelty — most portcos generate better returns from basic monitoring, external data sources, and dashboards operators actually use, rather than from AI infrastructure builds; (2) design for repeatability — sustainable value comes from embedding data into everyday decisions like pricing timing, early intervention in underperforming locations, and team retention signals; (3) use early wins to build toward a coherent data backbone. The report also notes that approaching exit, the market now expects portcos to be demonstrably AI-ready — PE buyers are scoring data infrastructure in diligence.

**What It Means for The Construct:**
This is a direct Hartley Capital playbook item: the "favor reliability over novelty" finding argues against over-engineering AI at the portco level during the hold period and in favor of lightweight, repeatable data infrastructure that scores well at exit diligence — the exact posture a disciplined roll-up operator should be building into every acquisition thesis. The exit-readiness AI score dynamic is new and time-sensitive: portcos not AI-ready by next diligence cycle will take a valuation discount.

**Executive Summary:**
KPMG's inaugural Global AI Pulse draws on 2,100+ senior leaders across 20 countries to map how enterprises are transitioning from fragmented AI use cases to orchestrated, agent-driven systems. The lead finding: organizations are projecting average AI spend of $207 million over the next 12 months — nearly double year-prior figures. But the constraint is not capital: requirements for human validation of agent outputs have nearly tripled year over year (63% now require human validation, up from 22% in Q1 2025), signaling that governance and trust are now operational bottlenecks, not aspirational concerns. The paper frames execution — not capital or technology — as the differentiator, with workforce readiness, governance, and operating models identified as the actual constraints on AI value. The shift toward orchestrated, enterprise-wide capability is accelerating faster than the governance infrastructure that would contain it.

**What It Means for The Construct:**
The human validation tripling is the most actionable data point for Hartley Capital's AI agent deployment strategy: the firms generating real returns in 2026 are not those running agents autonomously — they are those that designed the human-in-the-loop checkpoints carefully and early. Any Hartley Capital portfolio position deploying AI agents in operations, diligence, or fund admin should be audited against this governance standard immediately.

## 4. The TSMC Story (the day's dominant narrative)

TSMC reported a **record Q2**: net profit NT$706.6bn (~$22bn), +77% YoY; preliminary revenue $39.62bn, +36% YoY; AI chips were 61% of total sales. By any conventional measure, a blowout quarter.

Stock reaction: down anyway, dragging the entire chip complex — Asian semiconductor stocks lost an estimated **$1.3–1.4 trillion** in market value over the prior days heading into the print. The read from analysts: expectations had run so far ahead of results that "blowout" wasn't enough — this is a "priced-for-perfection" unwind, not a fundamentals problem. Elevated capex plans and margin questions added to the pressure.

**Read for the desk:** this is the single most important thread for today's session. Watch whether SOX/semis stabilize intraday in the US, or whether the "sell the news" reaction spreads into AI-capex-adjacent names (hyperscalers, power/utilities, data-center suppliers) that have run on the same narrative.

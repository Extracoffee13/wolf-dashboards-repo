## Why YELLOW and not RED or GREEN

Nothing observed indicates an actual 4xx/5xx, broken embed, or missing content on the live site — but nothing
observed *confirms* the site is healthy either, since the verification tooling itself failed. Calling this
GREEN would be a false-positive risk; calling it RED would be an unsupported false-negative. Flagging YELLOW
with an explicit "audit incomplete" reason keeps the report honest.

## Recommendation

Per the `b9-verify-and-index` skill, direct cloud/session `curl`/`fetch` to brand9signs.com is known to be
unreliable even when egress isn't blocked, due to an sgcaptcha bot-shield that returns a CAPTCHA redirect
instead of real content. Live verification of this site reliably requires either (a) a session with the
authenticated Claude-in-Chrome browser holding the sgcaptcha cookie, or (b) egress to brand9signs.com enabled
for this environment. Recommend re-running this daily check from a session with one of those two capabilities,
or routing it through the existing Cowork/Hermes verification path described in that skill.

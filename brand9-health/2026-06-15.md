## Detailed Findings

### Checks 1–5: HTTP 403 Blocking
All requests to the following endpoints returned `HTTP 403 Forbidden` with no response body:
- `https://brand9signs.com/` (homepage)
- `https://brand9signs.com/product-category/marketing-branding/`
- `https://brand9signs.com/product/motion-films/`
- `https://brand9signs.com/product/corporate-branding-signage/`
- `https://brand9signs.com/product/brand-identity-signs/`
- `https://brand9signs.com/product/graphics/`
- `https://brand9signs.com/robots.txt`
- `https://brand9signs.com/sitemap_index.xml`
- `https://brand9signs.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts` (REST API)

The uniform 403 across all paths (including robots.txt and sitemap) is characteristic of Cloudflare's Bot Fight Mode or Super Bot Fight Mode blocking the monitoring agent's user-agent string. The site is NOT returning 5xx server errors; it is actively denying bot access.

**Confirmation the site is live (via WebSearch / external indexing):**
- Yelp, Facebook, and RocketReach all list brand9signs.com as active.
- Google search returns current brand9signs.com URLs with valid descriptions.
- Product pages found indexed: Corporate Branding Signage, Brand Identity Signs, Graphics, Historic House Signs.
- The site has been publishing content in 2026 (North Florida HQ page dated May 2026; Brand Lab AI page active June 2026).

## Monitoring Blocker — Critical Note

**The site returns HTTP 403 to all automated monitoring tools.** This is the dominant finding of this audit. The WAF/security layer (likely Cloudflare) is blocking the monitoring user-agent across every URL tested — homepage, category pages, product pages, and sitemap.xml.

- Real-user traffic: unaffected (Googlebot can crawl; pages are indexed normally)
- Automated health monitoring: **completely blind**
- Impact: 5 of 6 checks are YELLOW due to inability to retrieve page content, not due to actual site defects

**Recommended action:** Add the monitoring tool's IP/user-agent to the WAF allowlist, or switch to a browser-based health check (e.g., Puppeteer/Playwright headless browser) that presents a normal browser fingerprint.

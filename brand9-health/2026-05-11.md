## Root Cause Assessment

The 403 responses are **uniform across all URL types** (homepage, category, product, sitemap, robots.txt), which is consistent with a **server-wide bot-protection rule** rather than a genuine site outage. Likely causes:

- **Cloudflare Bot Management** — blocks non-browser user-agents at the edge
- **Wordfence or similar WP security plugin** — blocks known crawler/automation user-agents
- **Server-level `.htaccess` rule** targeting automated agents

Since Google's index shows fresh, rich content and the site appears indexed normally, real browser traffic is almost certainly unaffected. However, this configuration **completely prevents automated health monitoring** and may also block:
- Uptime monitoring services
- SEO crawlers (Screaming Frog, SEMrush, Ahrefs)
- Social media OG scrapers (Facebook, Slack link previews may be impacted)

**Recommended action:** Whitelist known monitoring IPs/UAs or use a server-side health endpoint. Verify in WP admin that the Motion Films product page is published and indexed.

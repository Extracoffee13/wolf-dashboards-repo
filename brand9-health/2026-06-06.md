## Root Cause: Monitoring Blind Spot

The Cloudflare configuration on brand9signs.com is returning **HTTP 403** to the health checker's user-agent/IP range. This is the primary finding of today's run. Human visitors and Google's crawler are unaffected.

**Recommended fix:** Allowlist the monitoring tool's IP or user-agent in Cloudflare's security settings (Firewall Rules → allow specific UA or IP for health checks). Alternatively, use a WordPress-side health endpoint (e.g., a custom REST route with an auth token) that bypasses Cloudflare inspection.

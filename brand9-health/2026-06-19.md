## Root Cause: WAF Bot Protection Blocking Monitoring Tool

The consistent HTTP 403 across all URLs — including homepage, category pages, product pages, sitemap, and RSS feed — indicates the site's WAF (likely Cloudflare or similar) is fingerprinting and blocking the monitoring agent's user-agent or IP. This is a monitoring infrastructure problem, not necessarily a user-facing site outage.

**Recommended actions:**
1. **Whitelist the monitoring agent's IP/user-agent** in Cloudflare or the hosting WAF rules.
2. **Investigate the Motion Films product page** — it is not appearing in Google's index.
3. **Manual spot-check**: Verify the homepage motion film embed and the /product-category/marketing-branding/ nav/product grid manually in a browser.
4. Consider switching the monitoring fetch tool to a browser-based renderer (Puppeteer/Playwright) that mimics real user traffic.

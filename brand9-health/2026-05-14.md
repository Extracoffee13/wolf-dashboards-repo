## Detailed Findings

### 1–5 · Systematic HTTP 403 — WAF Blocking Health Agents

Every GET request to brand9signs.com — including `robots.txt`, `sitemap_index.xml`, the homepage, and five product URLs — returned **HTTP 403 Forbidden**. This is consistent with Cloudflare or equivalent WAF rules set to challenge/block non-browser user-agents.

- Site IS reachable to Googlebot (pages are being indexed, search snippets return current content).
- Site IS NOT reachable to automated monitoring agents, health checkers, or API-based fetch tools.
- **Impact:** No programmatic SLA monitoring is possible. Uptime dashboards, external synthetic monitors, and CI/CD health gates are all blind to this site.
- **Recommended fix:** Add IP ranges or user-agent exceptions for monitoring tools (UptimeRobot, Pingdom, etc.) in the WAF allow-list; or configure Cloudflare's "Known Bots" allow policy to permit uptime monitoring agents.

### 6 · Content Freshness — 🟡 YELLOW

Google's search index shows the most recently indexed content dated to **November–December 2025** (~6 months ago). No posts, products, or pages dated 2026 appear in the index. This could mean:
- No new content has been published since late 2025, or
- New content is being blocked from crawling by the same WAF rules, or
- Crawl budget is low and Googlebot hasn't re-indexed recent changes.

Either scenario warrants investigation.

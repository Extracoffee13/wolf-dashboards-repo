# First-Principles Backlog

Open questions for the daily First-Principles Spike. Pick one, spike it, then
either strike it here or leave it (spikes can be revisited from a different
angle later). Add new questions as they occur to you during other work.

## Open

- Why do most signage companies price jobs by cost-plus-per-square-foot
  rather than by value delivered (visibility, foot traffic lift, brand
  impact)? Is cost-plus actually irrational, or does it solve a real
  information/negotiation problem?
- What is the correct way to depreciate a custom-fabricated sign asset for
  tax vs. economic purposes, and why might the two diverge?
- For a multi-agent system (like this Construct), when does a shared
  blackboard/state store beat direct message-passing between agents, and
  when does it become a liability?
- What is the minimum viable governance structure for a holding company
  (like Hartley Capital) coordinating multiple operating businesses before
  bureaucracy costs exceed coordination benefits?
- Is "brand" a real economic asset with defensible first-principles value,
  or is it just a label for switching costs and habit that could be
  measured more directly?

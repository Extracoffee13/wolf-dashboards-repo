# First-Principles Backlog

Open questions for the daily First-Principles Spike. Pick one per run, then
remove it (or leave it — the spike agent will pick a fresh one if this list
goes stale). Add new questions here as they come up in Construct work.

## Open questions

- Why does the sign industry price permanent monument signs by square footage
  of face rather than by illuminated vs. non-illuminated construction cost?
- What is the actual load-bearing justification for a 30-mile trade area
  radius in retail site selection, versus a drive-time isoline?
- Why do multi-agent systems converge on a hub-and-spoke (orchestrator +
  subagents) topology rather than a mesh of peer agents?
- Is a triple-net (NNN) lease actually lower risk for a landlord than a
  gross lease, or does it just relocate the risk to a place harder to see?
- Why does commercial real estate cap-rate math treat cap rate as a single
  number instead of a term structure like a bond yield curve?

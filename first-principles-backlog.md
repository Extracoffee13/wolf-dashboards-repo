# First-Principles Question Backlog

Daily reasoning-gym backlog for the First-Principles Spike. Pick one open
question per run (oldest first, or whatever's most relevant to current
Construct work), reason it from primitives with no retrieval, then check it
against the corpus. Move answered questions to the bottom with a link to
their spike file. Add new questions as they come up — from Construct work,
from tangents in other spikes, from anything that turns out to need a
first-principles check rather than a lookup.

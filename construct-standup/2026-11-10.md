## 5. One non-obvious thing
**The Construct is producing; the Construct's memory is not receiving.** Yesterday's standup said "no activity. Silence is the signal." Yet 6 commits landed on 2026-11-09, and they are the same five routines with the same pattern as today. Every routine dutifully appends its PRAXIS block to `praxis-inbox.md` on *its own throwaway branch*, so each one sees an inbox containing only itself, and no reader ever sees them together. The loop runs open: writers believe they captured lessons, and readers believe nobody wrote any. Today's content shows the cost. Two routines working independently (signage pulse and hartley-watch) both landed on "homebuilder rate shock means stress-test Lennar signage revenue." A third (first-principles) produced the counter-move: per-sheet pricing and repeat refaces as the steadier margin. That cross-routine convergence is exactly what PRAXIS is meant to promote, and with branch-scattered inboxes it never can. **Fix:** have each routine also push its PRAXIS block to one shared branch (or `main`), or point distill and standup at `git log --all`.

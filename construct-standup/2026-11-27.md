## 5. One non-obvious thing I noticed
**Each routine starts from zero every day, so settled facts flip and dropped work comes back looking new.** Two examples today:
- **MCP Tasks flipped.** On 11/26 the skill sweep said "not GA" and the AI pulse said "conflicting". On 11/27 the sweep said "GA" (citing Anthropic's article) and the pulse said "beta, GA claim rejected". Both are careful, and both cite sources. Run alone, each looks rigorous. Together, the Construct holds two opposite official positions on whether a tool it plans to pilot has shipped.
- **The spike re-ran its own lost question.** Yesterday's backlog held "supplier minimums vs. whole sheets". It was lost overnight. Today the spike generated "why 4x8, and which sizes does it favor?" from scratch and marked it **rediscovered**. Part of what it rediscovered was its own dropped question. It still found something new (the 24x36 waste strip), but only because it happened to land on the same topic again.

Yesterday's standup said that without a shared merged record the Construct can't tell a real convergence from an echo. Today shows the cost of that: **without that record, it can't hold a conclusion either.** Each routine is rigorous on its own day and amnesiac across days, so carefulness becomes noise. The cheapest fix isn't another agent. It's one canonical facts file on `main` (for example, "MCP Tasks: beta|GA, source, date") that every routine reads before it writes. That's the same merge-to-`main` decision that has been waiting for Bobby since day 1.

- **arXiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture"** — [arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619) — Proposes context engineering as a discipline distinct from prompt engineering, with five context-quality criteria (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance) framing context as the agent's "operating system." Applicable to The Construct: **yes** — a useful checklist to audit our PRAXIS inbox / multi-agent handoff format against, since our agents already pass structured context blocks between each other.

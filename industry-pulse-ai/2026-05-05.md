- **arXiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (2603.09619) + "Architectural Design Decisions in AI Agent Harnesses" (2604.18071)**

The second is an empirical study of 70 agent-harness projects, surfacing common architectural failure modes.
  **Applicable to The Construct: YES** — CE framing directly informs how PRAXIS agent prompts and tool schemas should be structured; the harness paper's failure-mode taxonomy is actionable for hardening existing WOLF agents before scaling.

- **arXiv agent-architecture reads (no clearly dated Nov-2026 paper surfaced this week; best recent picks)**
  Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22367 (Reasoner-Executor-Synthesizer, O(1) context) · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03726 (SelfMem: self-optimizing agent memory) · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.39989 (Component replacement in LLM agents, scoping review)

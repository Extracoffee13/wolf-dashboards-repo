**5. arXiv: "Context Engineering" emerges as standalone discipline (arxiv 2603.09619)**
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619 | https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18071v1
- Two complementary papers codify context engineering (CE) as the discipline of structuring the full informational environment of an agent — encoding goals, values, and trade-off hierarchies into infrastructure rather than individual prompts — with empirical study of 70 agent systems confirming CE becomes infrastructural at scale.
- **Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly actionable: our PRAXIS prompt stack and agent harness configs are doing CE ad hoc; formalizing this would improve reliability and reduce prompt drift across Wolf agents.

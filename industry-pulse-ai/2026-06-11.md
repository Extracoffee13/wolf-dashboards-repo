## 5. arXiv: "Context Engineering — From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture"
**Source:** [arXiv:2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619)

Proposes five criteria for context quality in multi-agent systems — relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, and provenance — and frames the agent's context window as its operating system, with empirical grounding from enterprise deployments.

**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — The Construct's PRAXIS_INBOX pattern is a direct instance of "context economy + provenance." Applying the paper's isolation criterion would mean each agent (AP, Vector, Forge, etc.) gets only its relevant slice of shared state rather than a full inbox dump — worth a Keystone/Architect review of the inbox schema.

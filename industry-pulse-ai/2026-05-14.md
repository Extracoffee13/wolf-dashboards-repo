- **arXiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (2603.09619)**

  **Applicable to The Construct: YES** — the LGA framework maps cleanly onto The Construct's multi-agent trust boundaries; the context-engineering framing should inform how AP, Wolf, and Forge share state and tool access.

# Industry Pulse — AI/Agent Ecosystem — 2026-09-01

Applicable to The Construct: maybe — if any client runs Salesforce, this becomes a native agent surface for Ledger/Prism-style CRM work instead of a custom integration; worth a watch-item, not urgent.

Applicable to The Construct: yes — we already operate against Higgsfield via MCP for Vector's image/video pipeline; this confirms the vendor is investing in first-party MCP surface area rather than us maintaining a workaround, reducing future integration risk.

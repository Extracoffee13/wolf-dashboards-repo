Several come from aggregators and press releases rather than primary pages, and are marked *(unverified)*.

- **Multi-agent fan-out costs 4.7x the tokens for about +8 pts success (arXiv 2611.12953, Amazon/UW, Nov 7)** · https://fourweekmba.com/ai-amazon-multi-agent-ai-uses-4-7x-tokens-for-an-8-point-gain/ (abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12953). Companion paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.10022 ("How Much Skill Orchestration Can LLM Agents Compile Away?")
  On enterprise workflows, only one coordinated multi-agent design beat a single agent by a significant margin (75.6% vs 67.4%), and it used 4.7x the tokens. The single agent was cheapest on every axis. *(Numbers from a secondary write-up; the arXiv abstract was not opened.)*
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** Our V6 default is "Claude agents fan out and verify." This argues for gating fan-out by task value: single-agent by default, multi-agent only for high-stakes verification such as client decks or live-site writes. Read 2611.10022 too. Its idea of compiling repeated skill orchestration into specialized execution fits our very large skill library.

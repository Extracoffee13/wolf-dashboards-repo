## 5. arXiv: "Context Engineering" Reframes Agent Design (2603.09619)
**Source:** [arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619)
**Summary:** Vera Vishnyakova's paper formalizes *context engineering* as the successor to prompt engineering — a discipline for designing the full informational environment of multi-agent systems — proposing five quality criteria (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance) and framing context as the agent's operating system.
**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly applicable to architecting The Construct's agent pipelines; the five criteria provide a practical audit framework for diagnosing why agents drift or hallucinate in production, and the corporate multi-agent architecture section maps to The Construct's existing WOLF/Praxis topology.

# AI/Agent Ecosystem Pulse — 2026-07-10

- **Digital signage vendors now ship native MCP servers.** ([Digital Signage MCP Vendor Guide 2026](https://cast-hub.com/digital-signage-mcp-vendors/)) CastHub, Screenly, and Revel Digital have production-grade MCP servers letting Claude control signage CMS directly (content push, playlist/schedule management) via OAuth custom connectors; Fugo and NoviSign are in beta, Korbyt has a roadmap ETA of June 30, 2026.
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — directly in our vertical. Worth a spike connecting Claude to a CastHub/Screenly sandbox to see if it can replace manual playlist scheduling steps in our signage workflow.

- **Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5 (1M context, 128k max output) and expanded Cowork to mobile/web.** ([Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-brings-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-as-usage-data-shows-most-users-arent-coding)) Cowork now supports background work, scheduled tasks, shared chat/projects, and mobile approvals (Max beta first); Managed Agents gained event deltas, backward pagination, and vault injection controls.
  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — Cowork mobile approvals could streamline client sign-off loops; Managed Agents' vault injection controls are relevant if we centralize agent secrets management.

- **xAI released Grok 4.5 (July 8); Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image shipped from Google (June 23).** ([AI Release Tracker](https://aireleasetracker.com/latest)) Frontier labs continue near-3-day release cadence; no major architecture shift reported this week beyond incremental multimodal/efficiency gains.
  **Applicable to The Construct: no** — nothing actionable without a specific cost/latency need it solves better than current Claude usage.

- **Real estate data MCP servers matured (ATTOM, Cotality/CoreLogic, NexGenData).** ([ChatForest MCP/Real Estate guide](https://chatforest.com/guides/mcp-real-estate/), [Cotality MCP](https://www.cotality.com/platforms/mcp-server)) These connect agents to MLS/property/AVM data for underwriting and valuation workflows without enterprise contracts (NexGenData positions itself as a drop-in ATTOM/CoreLogic replacement).
  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — relevant if any real-estate-facing signage/dashboard client work needs live property data; otherwise low priority.

- **"Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (arXiv 2603.09619) and AgentSPEX (arXiv 2604.13346) advance agent-context design.** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619)) The context-engineering paper frames context as the agent's "operating system" with five quality criteria (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance); AgentSPEX proposes a formal spec/execution language for agents.
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — the five context-quality criteria are a useful rubric to audit our own agent prompts (Vector, Forge, Signal, etc.) for context bloat/isolation issues.

**5. arXiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (2603.09619)**
Source: [arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09619)
The paper argues that prompt engineering was designed for single request-response cycles and breaks under multi-step orchestration pressure; it proposes "context engineering" — structured context windows, state handoffs, and declarative capability registries — as the right abstraction for corporate multi-agent systems.
**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly frames how to evolve WOLF/Praxis from prompt-driven to context-engineered; the declarative capability registry pattern maps cleanly onto our MCP server inventory.

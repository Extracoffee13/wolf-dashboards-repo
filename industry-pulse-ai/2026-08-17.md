- **Claude Cowork's Chrome side panel is now a full session** — skills, plugins, and connectors work directly in the browser with no extra setup, and conversations persist across the panel. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/12/claude-cowork-chrome/), [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-plugins))
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — this is the exact surface several of our operator skills (b9-gbp-audit, chrome-automation-user-visibility, higgsfield-browser-studio) already depend on; worth re-testing our Cowork/Chrome workflows now that sessions persist natively in-panel instead of needing tab juggling.

([AIdapted](https://www.aidapted.ro/en/articles/ai-news-august-16-2026-openai-deepseek-europe/))
  **Applicable to The Construct: no** — we don't route production workloads through DeepSeek, but worth flagging if any cost-comparison decks reference DeepSeek as a "cheap" alternative — that argument just got much weaker.

  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — if we ever multi-model-route (e.g., cost-optimize Higgsfield/render pipelines across providers), OpenRouter-style gateways are now a Stripe product, not just an indie tool — changes vendor risk calculus.

- **Salesforce's Hosted MCP Servers reached general availability**, connecting AI tools directly to Marketing Cloud for campaign orchestration, content generation, and subscriber management. ([Martech Notes](https://www.martechnotes.com/salesforce-hosted-mcp-servers-are-available/))
  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe — how** — relevant if any client (B9 or HyperCube/STRUO) runs Salesforce Marketing Cloud; gives our agents a native MCP path into their CRM instead of custom API glue.

- **"Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture"** frames context management (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance) as a discipline distinct from prompt engineering, treating context as the agent's operating system. ([arXiv:2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619))
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — directly validates our multi-agent/PRAGMA architecture (Pod model, skill-scoped context, praxis-inbox as provenance log); useful vocabulary for documenting why we isolate context per-agent rather than sharing one giant prompt.

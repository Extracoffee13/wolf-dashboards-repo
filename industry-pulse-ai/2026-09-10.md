- **Frontier model cluster launch (72-hour window)** — [aireleasetracker.com](https://aireleasetracker.com/latest) / [local-ai-zone.github.io](https://local-ai-zone.github.io/blog/September_2026_AI_Model_Updates.html): Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.1 (Sept 1), Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash (Sept 2), Meta shipped Muse Spark 1.3 (Sept 2), OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3), and DeepSeek shipped DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Sept 10) — open-weight labs moved first, closed frontier labs followed within days. **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — Fable 5.1 cuts cache-read pricing to a quarter of other Claude models, worth checking against our current model routing/cost assumptions in agent configs.

- **Cowork "Customize" unification + Skills auto-loading** — [claude.com/blog/cowork-plugins-across-enterprise](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-plugins-across-enterprise): Anthropic merged plugins, skills, and connectors into one "Customize" admin surface, added starter-template plugin authoring guided by Claude, and shipped Skills auto-loading (Claude now auto-selects the right skill from task context instead of requiring exact invocation). **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — our skill library is large and manually invoked; auto-loading could reduce the overhead of remembering/triggering the right skill by name across agents.

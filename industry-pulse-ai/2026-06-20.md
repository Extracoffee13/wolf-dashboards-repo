## 5. "Context Engineering" — arXiv:2603.09619

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619v1

Paper formalizes _context engineering_ as a discipline separate from prompt engineering: designing the entire informational environment in which an agent makes decisions, with five quality criteria — relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance — and frames context as the agent's "operating system."

**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly maps to PRAXIS inbox design and Wolf agent prompt architecture; the five criteria provide an audit checklist for every agent system prompt and tool-result payload used in The Construct's automation stack.

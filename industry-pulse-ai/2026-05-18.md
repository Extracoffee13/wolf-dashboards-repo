## Top Findings

**1. Claude Opus 4.7 GA + Managed Agents "Dreaming" Memory & Multi-Agent Orchestration**
Source: [9to5Mac — Anthropic updates Claude Managed Agents with three new features](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/07/anthropic-updates-claude-managed-agents-with-three-new-features/)
Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.7 (87.6% SWE-Bench, 3x higher-res vision) and added a "dreaming" research preview that lets agents review past sessions to self-improve, plus a multi-agent orchestrator that delegates sub-jobs to parallel specialists on a shared filesystem.
**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — the dreaming/memory loop and multi-agent orchestration pattern maps directly to PRAXIS; would replace ad-hoc inter-agent handoff with a first-party, Anthropic-managed substrate.

**5. arxiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture"**
Source: [arxiv 2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09619)
Introduces *Intent Engineering (IE)* — encoding organizational goals, values, and trade-off hierarchies into agent infrastructure rather than individual prompts, enabling coherent behavior at scale across multi-agent systems.
**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — IE framing gives PRAXIS a principled design pattern: encode Construct strategy/values at the harness level so all agents inherit them without per-prompt repetition.

**3. Google Gemini Omni released May 19 — native multimodal, 2M token context**

**5. arxiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (2603.09619)**
- **Source:** [arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619)
- Proposes Context Engineering (CE) as the successor discipline to prompt engineering — five quality criteria (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance) for designing the informational environment of multi-agent systems, drawing on Google ADK, Anthropic, and LangChain architectures.
- **Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly applicable to WOLF's multi-agent orchestration design; the isolation + provenance criteria map cleanly onto our need to keep client data siloed across accounts.

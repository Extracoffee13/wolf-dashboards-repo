- **Bay ($50M) and Plurai ($13M seed, GA): agent-action firewall plus adversarial pre-production simulation (Nov 10–11).**
  https://thenextweb.com/news/bay-50m-ai-agent-security-stealth-battery-ventures · https://www.unite.ai/plurai-raises-13m-seed-and-opens-ai-agent-simulation-platform/

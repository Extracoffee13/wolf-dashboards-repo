**5. arXiv: "Context Engineering" reframes prompt engineering as an agent-OS discipline**
- Source: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619)
- Vera Vishnyakova (March 2026) introduces context engineering as a standalone field: designing and managing the *entire informational environment* an agent operates in, not just the prompt.

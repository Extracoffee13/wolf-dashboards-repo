1. **Claude Code mods and v2.1.287 (Oct 1).** Mods are TypeScript plugins that can rewrite prompts, add UI or replace built-in CLI/desktop features, and they ship via plugins. The release also adds URL prompts from MCP servers (2025-11-25 protocol), and servers that stop connecting need `"bareElicitationCapability": true`.
   https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude
   **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** Mods could host Construct/Praxis hooks (inbox capture, gatekeeper checks) inside Claude Code. Check that MCP servers still connect after the update.

2. **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Sep 28) and Claude for Government, GA.** Sonnet 5.5 is the new default mid-tier model. Claude for Government is FedRAMP High with audit logs and spend controls.
   https://www.anthropic.com/news
   **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** Route Claude-agent fan-out and verification to Sonnet 5.5 and compare cost and quality against current settings. The Government release has no direct use for us.

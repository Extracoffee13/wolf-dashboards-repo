# Industry Pulse — AI/Agent Ecosystem — 2026-09-20

- **Anthropic discloses Claude now drives 26% of its own model R&D, ~30,000 agents running concurrently internally** ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/news)) — Anthropic revealed on Sep 18 that Claude's share of its own R&D work rose from 0% in February to 26% in August 2026, with tens of thousands of Claude agents running in parallel inside the company. **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — direct validation that our multi-agent swarm model (Hermes fleet, PRAXIS inbox pattern) is the same trajectory the frontier lab itself is betting on; worth revisiting our agent-concurrency ceilings.

- **ElevenLabs MCP server extended to ElevenCreative — voice, music, image, and video generation in one MCP endpoint** ([source](https://www.testingcatalog.com/elevenlabs-mcp-can-now-generate-voice-music-images-video/)) — ElevenLabs folded its full ElevenCreative suite (50+ models) into its existing MCP server, so Claude/Cursor/ChatGPT sessions can generate speech, music, SFX, images, and video without leaving the conversation. **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — overlaps with our Higgsfield MCP integration; worth a side-by-side eval for voice/music gen specifically since Higgsfield's strength is video/3D, not audio.

- **DAM platforms race to ship native MCP servers (Aprimo "Agentic DAM," Frontify, Cloudinary, Acquia)** ([source](https://www.thedroptimes.com/71839/acquia-dam-mcp-server-ai-assistants)) — Digital asset management vendors are converging on MCP as the standard layer for AI-agent access to brand/creative asset libraries, with governance and approval controls built in. **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — this is the exact pattern our signage/render asset pipelines (B9, VironBox, Struo) need for agent-safe asset retrieval; an MCP-native DAM could replace ad hoc file-search workflows.

- **Anthropic merges Claude Cowork into Claude Chat, dropping to two surfaces (Chat, Code)** ([source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/16/anthropic-merging-claude-cowork-with-chat/)) — rolling out over the coming weeks for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise, folding Cowork's plugin/connector model directly into chat rather than keeping it a separate mode. **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — several of our workflows are built around Cowork-specific plugin bundling; need to confirm our installed knowledge-work plugins survive the merge unchanged.

- **New arxiv paper: "Harness Engineering: Anatomy, Architecture, and Evolution of Coding Agents"** ([source](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.SE/2026-09)) — a source-code study of eleven coding-agent harnesses (expanded from an April 2026 study), mapping how agent harnesses structure tool access, context, and control flow. **Applicable to The Construct: maybe** — directly relevant background reading for anyone iterating on our own agent harness/skill design conventions, though not an immediate action item.

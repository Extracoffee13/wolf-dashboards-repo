**1. Anthropic ships Claude Opus 4.7 + Memory for Managed Agents (public beta)**
> Source: [anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7)

**5. arxiv: "Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture" (2603.09619)**

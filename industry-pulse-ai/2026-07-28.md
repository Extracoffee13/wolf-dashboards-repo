- **"Context Engineering: From Prompts to Corporate Multi-Agent Architecture"** — [arXiv:2603.09619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619) — Proposes context engineering as a discipline distinct from prompt engineering, framing context as the agent's "operating system" and defining five quality criteria (relevance, sufficiency, isolation, economy, provenance) for multi-agent systems. **Applicable to The Construct: yes** — directly relevant framework for auditing how context flows between C-Suite agents (Praxis inbox, WOLF live data, skill packets).

# Industry Pulse — AI/Agent Ecosystem — 2026-11-23

Window: last 24h, widened to about 7 days because the 24h window was thin (it was the weekend, and no Claude Code release shipped Nov 21–23). Sources are mostly trade press and aggregator sites, not checked against vendor primary posts unless noted.

- **Anthropic: computer use beta for Team/Enterprise (Nov 20) and self-hosted Managed Agents on the Developer Platform (Nov 12).** Claude can now drive desktop apps on Team and Enterprise plans, and Managed Agents can run on our own infrastructure. Claude Code 2.1.328 (Nov 19) also made auto mode the default for Bedrock, Vertex and Foundry sessions.
  Sources: https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude · https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-developer-platform · https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** Self-hosted Managed Agents could run Hermes-style workers on the agent Mac with an Anthropic-managed loop. Computer use is a fallback for vendor portals (Signs365, Gemini, WS Display) where browser tools freeze. Worth a pilot.

- **Frontier labs, busy week: Google shipped Gemini 4 Neon (Nov 18, mid-tier, about $3/$15 per M tokens), and OpenAI shipped GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol (Nov 17, Astra 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra).** Alibaba's Qwen 4 Max and Tencent's Hy4 Flash launched Nov 16. xAI and DeepSeek had no November releases.
  Sources: https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/google-releases-gemini-4-neon-051643298.html · https://llmgateway.io/timeline
  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe.** Run Gemini 4 Neon and GPT-6.2 through the b9-ai-shelf measurement, because new versions of the answer engines can change whether Brand 9 is named. Cheaper mid-tier models are also options for bulk alt-text and copy passes.

- **MCP servers: Proof launched an x401 agent-authorization MCP in ChatGPT, Zinc launched a hosted retail-ordering MCP (both Nov 18), and Man of Many launched a public read-only publisher MCP (Nov 23).** These servers let an agent prove who it acts for and what that person approved, place real retail orders, and cite a publisher's content.
  Sources: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/18/3405765/0/en/proof-launches-its-mcp-in-chatgpt-starting-with-x401-so-ai-agents-can-prove-who-they-act-for-and-what-that-person-approved.html · https://www.prweb.com/releases/zinc-launches-hosted-mcp-server-and-retail-order-apis-for-ai-shopping-agents-in-2026-302942962.html · https://www.mi-3.com.au/23-11-2026/man-many-launches-public-mcp-server-australian-digital-publishing-first
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** A public, read-only "Brand 9 product and FAQ" MCP server, built like Man of Many's, would put the shop catalog straight into ChatGPT and Claude (a new AI-shelf channel). Approval-attestation servers like x401 fit our owner-approval gates on supplier orders. No new signage, DAM or real-estate MCP servers were found.

- **Agent fundraise: Model ML raised $250M at a $1.5B valuation (Nov 17–19) for vertical agents serving bankers and consultants.** Everful raised $48.5M for supply-chain agents for small retailers.
  Sources: https://thenextweb.com/news/model-ml-250m-series-b-1-5bn-valuation-ai-agents-bankers · https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/ai-startup-for-bankers-raises-funds-at-1-5-billion-value
  **Applicable to The Construct: maybe.** It supports the thesis that agents built for one industry win (CLEARHOUSE, the B9 bid engine). Everful's model, with agents that vet factories and manage shipping, is close to our supplier-ordering automation.

- **arXiv papers (Nov 2026):** (1) *Steering Emergent Behavior in Multi-Agent LLM Systems via Information Networks*: who observes whom is a design choice. (2) *Signed but Not Safe: Requirement Integrity in Multi-Agent LLM Handoffs*: requirements drift when work passes between agents. (3) *Multi-LLM Agentic AI at the Edge*: a supervisor splits requests into sub-tasks and schedules them on workers.
  Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11714 · https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current · https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20915
  **Applicable to The Construct: yes.** Papers 1 and 2 bear directly on the JEV → Hermes → Claude fan-out. Pass requirements to workers as typed, checked specs rather than free text, and decide on purpose which agents see which outputs.

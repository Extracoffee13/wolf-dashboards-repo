- **arXiv this cycle (no 2611 IDs surfaced yet; most recent are Oct):** CEPO (2610.34722) splits a shared coordinator prompt from per-agent executor prompts. *Not Every Agent Needs the Strongest Model* (2610.31743) jointly optimizes prompts and model choice per agent for cost. *Reflective Prompt Optimization of a Deep-Research Agent* (2610.12601) shows prompt-optimizer gains need held-out topics and multiple seeds to be credible.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34722 · https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31743 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12601
  **Applicable: YES.** CEPO maps directly onto JEV (coordinator) vs Hermes workers (executors). 2610.31743 supports routing cheap models to routine Hermes tasks. 2610.12601 is a caution for b9-change-measurement-loop: don't trust single-seed prompt wins.

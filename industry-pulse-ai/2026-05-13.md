**5. arXiv: "Architectural Design Decisions in AI Agent Harnesses" (Apr 2026)**
Source: [https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18071v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18071v1)
Systematic analysis of how production agent harnesses handle context retention, compression, and reintroduction across turns — concluding that context management is an *infrastructure* problem, not a prompt-engineering problem, at scale.
**Applicable to The Construct: YES** — directly informs how PRAXIS/Wolf harness should handle long-running agent sessions; suggests externalising context to a vector store rather than relying on in-window compression.

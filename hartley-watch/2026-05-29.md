**1. Florida housing inventory reaches pre-pandemic norms**
- **Source:** [Florida Realtors — May 2026](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/05/florida-housing-inventory-near-pre-pandemic-norms-economist-says)
- **What happened:** FL statewide inventory hit 4.7-month supply (lowest in a year); median SF price $420K (+1.8% YoY, 2.3% below Apr-2024 all-time high); Jacksonville stabilizing ~4-month supply with 50 days on market.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Validates the cautious-but-not-bearish FL RE thesis — the correction phase is largely over and secondary metros (Jacksonville, Tampa) are entering a window where basis risk is more calculable; worth revisiting any builder-land or BTR underwriting we've been holding.

**2. Sierra AI closes $950M at $15B valuation for B2B agent platform**
- **Source:** [SiliconANGLE — May 4, 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/04/ai-agent-startup-sierra-valued-15b-new-950m-funding-round/)
- **What happened:** Sierra — an enterprise AI agent platform focused on customer service — raised $950M at a $15B valuation, one of the largest pure-play agentic-AI rounds to date.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** The Construct's competitive moat is narrowing fast at the generalist layer; the wedge must be vertical specificity (homebuilder workflows, signage ops) rather than agent infrastructure per se — a $15B gorilla owns the plumbing; The Construct must own the domain.

- **What happened:** D.R.

- **Florida Realtors: "Florida inventory tells a split-market story" (7/20/2026)** — [floridarealtors.org](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/07/florida-inventory-tells-split-market-story)
  - **What happened:** Statewide inventory is diverging by metro — Jacksonville has flipped to a ~6-month buyer's market (median price down 4.4% YoY to $282K), Orlando/Lakeland show the largest surpluses, while Miami remains the one major market still below pre-pandemic supply norms.
  - **Hartley Capital implication:** Validates a metro-by-metro (not statewide) view of FL real estate risk — Jacksonville softness could pressure builder-client signage budgets there sooner than in tighter Miami/Naples markets; worth segmenting any FL-exposed thesis by metro going forward.

- **AI agent funding: B2B enterprise-automation agents captured 58% of $1.8B deployed in July 2026** — [AI Funding: AI Agent Startup Funding July 2026](https://aifunding.me/insights/ai-agent-funding-july-2026)
  - **What happened:** Vertical, revenue-proven ($25M+ ARR) B2B agent startups are pulling premium Series B+ rounds (avg. $150M, valuations up 40% QoQ) — capital is rotating away from horizontal assistants toward narrow, ROI-provable agents.
  - **Hartley Capital implication:** Competitive-position signal for The Construct — the market is rewarding narrow vertical-agent plays with clear ROI over broad platforms, which favors The Construct's wedge-specific agent approach but also means well-funded vertical competitors could emerge faster in adjacent niches (e.g., signage ops, home-services agents).

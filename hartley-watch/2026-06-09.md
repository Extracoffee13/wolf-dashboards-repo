## 2. Florida Housing Bifurcation — Tampa Suburban Risk vs. Jacksonville/Miami Resilience

**Source:** [Norada Real Estate — FL Forecast](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/) | [Heart Mortgage Blog](https://blog.heartmortgage.com/post/2026-florida-housing-market-trends-2025-2026-median-home-price-inventory-days-on-market)

**What happened:** Florida condo inventory reached 9.7 months supply (deep buyer's market); Tampa suburban markets flagged for potential -8.9% price declines in overbuilt pockets. Jacksonville remains resilient at ~4 months supply; Miami luxury holding at +1.1–2.2% YoY. Statewide mortgage rates 6.0–6.4%; homeowners insurance now averaging ~$11,759/yr (+7% YoY), a persistent affordability headwind.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Any single "Florida RE" underwriting thesis is now too blunt. Jacksonville and Miami luxury remain constructive; Tampa condo/suburban new-construction exposure warrants mark-to-model scrutiny. Insurance cost escalation is a durable carry drag — residential RE theses in FL must price this in explicitly.

## 3. AI Agent Infrastructure Consolidating — 20.7% of 2026 YTD AI Deals in Execution Layer

**Source:** [New Market Pitch — Agentic AI Funding Trends](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/agentic-ai-funding-trends) | [Fundraise Insider — Saris $28.8M Series A](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/saris-raises-28-8m-series-a-for-bank-ai-agent-automation/)

**What happened:** Agent Execution Infrastructure captured 20.7% of all 2026 YTD AI deals (runtimes, sandboxes, observability, identity, security). Agent Development Platforms rebounded to ~$124M across 5 deals through May. B2B vertical AI agents are raising 4× more than consumer plays. Notable: Saris raised $28.8M Series A (8VC lead) for back-office bank agent automation.

**Hartley Capital implication:** The Construct's competitive threat is sharpening at the infrastructure layer — well-capitalized infra players will commoditize execution primitives within 12–18 months. The defensible wedge for The Construct is vertical distribution and domain-specific workflow depth (not the runtime). If The Construct is not yet differentiated on vertical lock-in, now is the window to build or partner before infra players move up-stack.

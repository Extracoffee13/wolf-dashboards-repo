# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-30

- **Source:** [D.R. Horton lowers FY2026 guidance](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/07/60574774/americas-largest-homebuilder-says-buyers-are-still-hesitating)
  **What happened:** D.R. Horton beat Q3 EPS estimates but cut FY26 revenue guidance to $32.5–33.0B (from $33.5–34.5B) and trimmed closings guidance to 83,800–84,300 homes (from 86,000–87,500) on persistent affordability pressure and buyer hesitation.
  **Hartley Capital implication:** Validates the caution thesis on homebuilder-adjacent revenue (signage, community-entry monument work) — largest national builder is pulling back closings forecasts, a leading indicator for slower new-community sign orders from Brand 9's builder clients over the next 2-3 quarters.

- **Source:** [Florida Housing Market Tracker — July 2026](https://movewithmomentum.com/data/florida-housing-tracker)
  **What happened:** Statewide Florida median home value hit a fresh cycle low of $392,443 (−3.0% YoY), inventory is down 10.9% YoY, and 25.2% of active listings carry price cuts — even as Sarasota/Naples inventory improves toward balance (4–5 months' supply).
  **Hartley Capital implication:** Confirms the "soft-landing, not crash" read on FL housing — price softness plus falling inventory (not rising) argues against a demand collapse for real-estate-adjacent signage work, but supports underwriting new deals conservatively on price appreciation assumptions.

- **Source:** [Norm AI raises $120M Series C, hits unicorn valuation — TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/ai-law-startup-norm-raises-120m-hits-unicorn-valuation/)
  **What happened:** Legal-AI agent startup Norm AI raised a $120M Series C led by Khosla Ventures at a $1.2B valuation, backed by Blackstone, Bain, Coatue and others, on an outcome-based (not hourly) pricing model for AI-agent-delivered legal services.
  **Hartley Capital implication:** New wedge signal for The Construct — a vertical B2B agent company is proving outcome-based pricing can command unicorn valuations; worth testing whether The Construct's agent-delivered services (vs. seat/subscription pricing) could adopt a similar outcome-priced model to differentiate from agent-infra competitors.

**1. Florida Inventory Surge Accelerates in Core Markets**

- **Source:** [Redfin Tampa Housing Market](https://www.redfin.com/city/18142/FL/Tampa/housing-market) · [Yahoo News — Orlando Market Cools](https://www.yahoo.com/news/orlando-real-estate-market-cools-172409243.html)
- **What happened:** Active inventory in Tampa (+92.7% YoY) and Orlando (+81.8% YoY) continued surging through May 2026; statewide median hit $395,595 (+1.7% YoY) but days-on-market widened (Tampa: 47 days vs. 42 last year), and Jacksonville is sitting at ~4 months of supply. Major builders (D.R. Horton, PulteGroup, Taylor Morrison) are escalating mortgage-rate buydowns to clear lots.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Validates a cautious near-term posture on Florida residential RE — a buyer's market is forming in the exact metros we track. Escalating builder incentive spend compresses margins on homebuilder client accounts (Brand 9 signage revenue tied to community opens); simultaneously, rising inventory could create a distressed-lot acquisition window before any rate relief re-ignites demand.

**2. Sign-Industry PE Roll-Up Intensifies — Allegra Absorbs Image360 Franchise; Stratus Acquires Comet Signs & MLE**

- **Source:** [ASI Central — Allegra Acquires Image360 Baltimore](https://members.asicentral.com/news/industry-news/march-2026/allegra-marketing-print-mail-baltimore-acquires-image360-baltimore-downtown/) · [Tracxn — Stratus Acquisitions](https://tracxn.com/d/acquisitions/acquisitions-by-stratus/__pjLPX6t3R9L_j5NxVgd-Hh93qGVEd-tG3eIZvtlRBsA)
- **What happened:** Alliance Franchise Brands' Allegra arm absorbed an Image360 franchise in Baltimore (March 2026), folding print/mail/signage under one roof. Concurrently, Stratus completed acquisitions of Priority, Comet Signs, and MLE — three more regional sign shops absorbed into its PE-backed platform.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Confirms the fragmented sign sector is consolidating faster than previously modeled. Both Alliance Franchise Brands and Stratus are using franchisee-level roll-ups to build density; Brand 9's independent positioning becomes more exposed without a clear technology or vertical moat. These deal comps can also anchor a valuation narrative if a Brand 9 exit thesis is being developed.

**3. Wonderful Closes $150M Series B at $2B Valuation — "Hyper-Local Enterprise AI Platform" Positioning Overlaps The Construct's Wedge**

- **Source:** [Index Ventures — Wonderful $150M Series B](https://www.indexventures.com/perspectives/index-ventures-triples-down-as-wonderful-raises-150m-series-b-to-scale-its-hyper-local-enterprise-ai-platform/)
- **What happened:** Amsterdam-based Wonderful (enterprise AI agent platform) closed a $150M Series B led by Insight Partners on March 12, 2026, reaching a $2B valuation just 8 months post-stealth with 900 employees across 30 global markets. The company explicitly frames itself as a "hyper-local enterprise AI platform" — the same positioning The Construct is building toward.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Surfaces a direct competitive signal for The Construct: a well-capitalized ($284M total raised) player is moving fast on near-identical positioning. The window for The Construct to lock in vertical-specific differentiation (e.g., signage ops, homebuilder workflows) is narrowing. Hartley should pressure-test whether The Construct's moat is domain-specific enough to survive a well-funded horizontal entrant.

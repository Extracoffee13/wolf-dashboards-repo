# Hartley Capital Watch: 2026-11-10

Window: the last 24h, with the most recent dated items used where no 24h news existed.

- **Homebuilders sold off on a hot inflation print.** Source: https://bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/dow-jones/2026/11/10/wall-street-mixed-in-early-trading-ahead-of-a-new-peek-at-inflation
  - **What happened:** Inflation came in hotter than expected on Nov 10. Lennar fell 4.4%, D.R. Horton 3.6% and PulteGroup 3.5%. That followed Lennar's 5.4% drop on Nov 3 and a short rally after the midterms.
  - **Hartley Capital implication:** This hits the Brand 9 / homebuilder-client thesis. If rates stay high, builders are likely to protect margins with incentives and slower land starts, and that delays new-community signage orders. Stress-test Q1 2027 Lennar division signage revenue and treat repeat refaces of existing communities as the steadier revenue. Validates diversifying beyond any single builder.

- **Florida resale inventory is down about 10% YoY while sales slow.** Source: https://www.letsmovetofla.com/2026/11/02/florida-housing-market-september-2026-home-sales-slow-prices-hold-steady-as-inventory-tightens
  - **What happened:** September statewide single-family median was $415K (+1.2% YoY). Single-family inventory fell 9.8% YoY and condo inventory 9%. New pending single-family sales dropped 7.6%. Condos keep softening (median $293.5K).
  - **Hartley Capital implication:** Tight resale supply plus slower sales keeps buyers looking at new construction, so builder model-park and community signage demand should hold up better than resale volume suggests. Condo weakness argues against condo-exposed FL real estate plays for now.

- **Agent-infra layers are being funded as standalone categories.** Sources: https://thenextweb.com/news/bay-50m-ai-agent-security-stealth-battery-ventures · https://thenextweb.com/news/graphon-labs-35m-series-a-ai-agent-memory · https://fintech.global/2026/11/09/pliant-raises-80m-as-ai-agents-reshape-b2b-payments/
  - **What happened:** Bay raised $50M for agent action security (allow, block or ask a person), Graphon Labs $35M for persistent agent memory, and Pliant $80M to open B2B payments to outside agents over MCP.
  - **Hartley Capital implication:** Security, memory and payments are turning into funded commodity layers. The Construct should integrate them rather than build them, and keep its moat in vertical operations (sign shop, homebuilder workflows) and orchestration. MCP-native payments could also open a wedge: agents paying suppliers directly within caps.

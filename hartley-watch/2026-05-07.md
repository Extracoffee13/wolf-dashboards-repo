## 1. Homebuilder Earnings Diverge — PulteGroup & NVR Miss Hard, D.R. Horton Holds

**Source:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/homebuilder-earnings-d-r-horton-144000677.html

**What happened:** Q1 2026 homebuilder earnings revealed a sharp split: NVR revenue fell 21.7% to $1.91B (missed $2.09B estimate), EPS down 28.6%; PulteGroup escalated buyer incentives to 10.9% of gross sale price (~$54,500 on a $500K home). D.R. Horton outperformed peers by leaning into entry-level product. Lennar targets ~85,000 deliveries in 2026 despite margin compression from 6% mortgage rates.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Validates a thesis to watch — Brand 9's homebuilder clients (Pulte, NVR-tier builders) are under margin stress and incentive inflation is a leading indicator of community-pipeline slowdowns. Entry-level builder resilience (DHI) is a potential hedge; Brand 9 exposure to premium-tier builders warrants a closer look at forward signage order flow.

## 2. Allegra Acquires Image360 Baltimore Downtown — Franchise-Level Roll-Up Accelerates

**Source:** https://members.asicentral.com/news/industry-news/march-2026/allegra-marketing-print-mail-baltimore-acquires-image360-baltimore-downtown/

**What happened:** Allegra Marketing Print Mail Baltimore acquired Image360 Baltimore Downtown (March 2026), consolidating print, mail, marketing, signage, and visual communications under one locally owned operator. Both are franchisees of Alliance Franchise Brands, the parent of Allegra, Image360, and related print/sign concepts.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Franchisee-level consolidation within Alliance Franchise Brands is a new data point — the roll-up is happening bottom-up (owner-operators combining units) not just top-down (PE buying platforms). This could compress independent sign-shop acquisition multiples if Alliance accelerates this playbook; it also surfaces Alliance as a more active competitive consolidator than previously tracked.

## 3. Basis Raises $100M at $1.15B — Vertical B2B Agent Unicorns Are Now Recurring News

**Source:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/basis-raises-100m-1-15b-150000934.html

**What happened:** Basis, an AI agent platform purpose-built for accounting firms (covering accounting, tax, and audit workflows), raised $100M Series B at a $1.15B valuation, led by Accel with GV (Google Ventures) and Lloyd Blankfein participating. Separately, Wonderful (customer-service agents for telecom, finance, healthcare, manufacturing) raised $150M Series B at $2B.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Vertical-specific B2B agent platforms are hitting unicorn status at Series B — this is the exact competitive stratum The Construct operates in. The Basis round confirms that professional-services verticals (accounting, legal, AEC) are venture-grade wedges. The Construct's differentiation thesis needs sharper articulation of its moat vs. well-funded vertical entrants; this is a validation of the market size but also a competitive pressure signal worth tracking quarterly.

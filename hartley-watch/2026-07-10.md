# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-10

- **Source:** [A major housing bill is set to become law at midnight — even though Trump says he won't sign](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/10/nx-s1-5885027/housing-bill-without-trump-signature) (NPR, 2026-07-10)
  **What happened:** The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act (passed Senate 85-5, House 358-32) becomes law at midnight tonight by default — Trump received it June 29 and let the 10-day signing clock lapse without signing or vetoing. It streamlines homebuilding regulation/environmental review and bars institutional investors holding 350+ single-family homes from buying more existing homes.
  **Hartley implication:** Directly validates the homebuilder-tailwind thesis — deregulation pressure plus a partial exit of institutional buyers from the existing-home market should favor new-construction volume, which is upside for Brand 9's homebuilder clients' community pipelines and a secondary tailwind for FL signage demand tied to new developments.

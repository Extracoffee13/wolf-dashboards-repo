**1. Sierra AI raises $950M at $15B valuation (May 4, 2026)**

**2. PulteGroup Q2 earnings: sales incentives hit 10.9% of gross sales price (April 23, 2026)**

Watch for Q3 capex reallocation toward model-home and community branding budgets.

**3. Tampa housing: suburban price risk of –8.9%, inventory inflecting to buyer-friendly (May 2026)**
- **Source:** [Houzeo — Florida Housing Market 2026](https://www.houzeo.com/housing-market/florida) / [Norada Real Estate — Florida Housing Market Forecast](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/)
- **What happened:** Tampa has been flagged as the Florida metro with the highest near-term price correction risk — up to –8.9% in overbuilt suburban pockets — driven by rising inventory and affordability pressure. Statewide inventory sits at 212,904 homes (5 months supply, –7.3% YoY), while Miami outperforms (+6.6% sales YoY for seventh consecutive month) and Jacksonville holds at ~4.5 months supply.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Divergence within Florida is the key signal: Tampa softness creates distressed-acquisition opportunities if Hartley holds dry powder for opportunistic RE; Miami/Jacksonville resilience supports thesis-consistent exposure in those submarkets. For any signage or brand-services portfolio companies serving Tampa builders, expect a near-term slowdown in community-launch and new-development project flow.

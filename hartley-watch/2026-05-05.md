**3. Florida RE spring 2026 bifurcation: Tampa suburban -8.9%, Naples -9% YoY; Jacksonville and Miami stabilizing**

- **Source:** [Truvera Title — FL Market Update Spring 2026](https://www.truveratitle.com/post/florida-real-estate-market-update-spring-2026) · [Norada Real Estate — FL Housing Forecast](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/)

- **Hartley Capital implication:** FL real estate positions require submarket-level re-underwriting. Coastal/luxury exposure (Naples, Sarasota) is still correcting and carries continued downside risk. The near-term opportunity lies in Jacksonville and Orlando suburban corridors where demand is stabilizing and builders are active — this aligns with where Brand 9 homebuilder clients (DHI, Orlando-area communities) are deploying new communities.

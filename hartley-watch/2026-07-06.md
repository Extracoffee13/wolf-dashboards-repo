# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-06

- **Florida real estate / rates**: 30-year fixed purchase rates are holding at 6.40% (15-year at 5.86%) as of today, with FL median home price still ~1.2% under its April 2024 all-time high ($430K) and homes taking ~43 days to go under contract; sellers are increasingly offering price cuts, closing-cost credits, and rate buydowns. ([NORADA, "Today's Mortgage Rates, July 6"](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/todays-mortgage-rates-july-6-2026-update/))
  *Implication*: Confirms the "buyer's market, slow grind" thesis for FL residential — softening affordability + rising incentive spend supports caution on any FL homebuilder-adjacent or resi-services position; validates watching insurance-cost drag as a distinct variable from rates.

- **Homebuilder client signal (Lennar)**: Lennar cut FY2026 guidance after a weak spring selling season — consensus EPS estimates cut ~24% (from $9.24 to $7.04), gross margin guidance fell to 15–16% (lowest since Q3 FY2009), average sales price down 4.6% YoY to $371K, and incentive spend running ~12.9% of final sales price. ([Benzinga, "Americans Still Can't Afford to Buy — and Homebuilder Stocks Are Paying the Price"](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-stock-ratings/analyst-color/26/07/60227792/americans-still-cant-afford-to-buy-and-homebuilder-stocks-are-paying-the-price))
  *Implication*: Direct read-through to Brand 9's homebuilder client base — margin compression this severe typically precedes marketing/signage budget tightening; worth flagging to any client-facing team ahead of Q3 renewal conversations, and a data point against overweighting homebuilder-linked revenue in near-term forecasts.

- **AI agent competitive landscape**: Microsoft announced "Microsoft Frontier Company" on July 2 — a new $2.5B-backed operating business with ~6,000 staff dedicated to delivering enterprise AI agent deployments using Microsoft's own tools. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-deployment-company-with-2-5-billion-commitment/))
  *Implication*: A hyperscaler now competing directly in enterprise agent *deployment/services*, not just infrastructure — raises the bar on The Construct's differentiation story (vertical specificity, speed, cost) versus a well-capitalized generalist; worth a wedge review on whether to lean harder into a niche Microsoft is unlikely to prioritize.

**No notable item**: No fresh (24h) M&A or PE roll-up activity surfaced for FastSigns/Propelled Brands, Image360/Alliance Franchise Brands, Stratus, or Allegra; most recent related transaction (Allegra Baltimore acquiring Image360 Baltimore Downtown) dates to March 2026.

# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-16

- **Homebuilder client pipeline softening.** Lennar's Q2 FY2026 8-K (filed with SEC) shows new orders down 4% YoY to 21,749 homes and backlog down to 16,818 homes ($6.6B), while D.R. Horton and Pulte guidance both flag incentives staying elevated through FY2026, compressing margins. Source: [Lennar 8-K, SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/920760/000162828026042551/ex991-2026531x8kq1.htm)
  *Hartley implication:* Direct exposure signal for Brand9 — softer builder order books and pipeline compression tend to lag into slower new-community signage/monument work 1–2 quarters out; worth flagging as a watch item on the homebuilder-client revenue thesis, not yet an alarm.

- **Florida market is bifurcating by metro.** Florida Realtors' July 2026 inventory read shows Orlando and Lakeland running the largest inventory surpluses (buyer's-market conditions), Tampa still tight at 3.9 months' supply, Jacksonville normalizing to ~4 months, and Miami the only major FL metro still below pre-pandemic inventory norms. Source: [Florida Realtors, "Florida inventory tells a split-market story"](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/07/florida-inventory-tells-split-market-story)
  *Hartley implication:* Validates a metro-selective posture rather than a blanket FL-growth thesis — Tampa/Miami tightness supports continued signage demand there, while Orlando/Lakeland surplus suggests builder activity (and downstream signage spend) is more likely to soften first in those submarkets.

- **AI agent funding is accelerating and consolidating around vertical/infra plays.** July 2026 saw ~$1.8B across 12+ agent-related deals, including Norm AI's $120M Series C (Khosla, with Blackstone/Vanguard/Bain Capital Ventures participating) and reports of an agent-run $100M raise by Lyzr; "Agent Execution Infrastructure" (runtimes, sandboxes, identity, observability) is now ~20% of 2026 YTD deal volume. Source: [TechCrunch, "An AI agent startup just let its agent run its $100M fundraise"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/)
  *Hartley implication:* New wedge signal — capital is flowing hard into agent infra/ops, not just vertical agents, which is directly adjacent to The Construct's positioning; worth a deeper look at whether "agent ops for SMB/franchise networks" (an underserved segment vs. the enterprise-heavy raises above) is still white space.

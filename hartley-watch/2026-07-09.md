# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-09

## 1. Prime Intellect raises $130M Series A at $1B valuation for enterprise AI-agent infrastructure
- **Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/prime-intellect-raises-130m-series-a-to-help-enterprises-build-their-own-ai-agents/
- **What happened:** Prime Intellect closed a $130M Series A (Radical Ventures, Nvidia Ventures, Intel Capital, Dell Technologies Capital, Iconiq, plus AI-founder angels) to offer a "full stack" of compute, RL training, and evaluation tooling so enterprises can build their own AI agents.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Validates the enterprise-agent-infra thesis The Construct sits in — a $1B-valuation infra layer targeting the same "let enterprises build agents" wedge is a build-vs-buy competitor to watch; worth monitoring whether it pushes into vertical/agent-ops territory that overlaps The Construct's positioning.

## 2. South Florida housing inventory keeps tightening even as prices stabilize statewide
- **Source:** https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/07/08/south-floridas-1-6-trillion-housing-market-fuels-regional-wealth/
- **What happened:** South Florida's owner-occupied housing stock is now valued at $1.6T (avg. sale price $893,900, +7.1% since Dec 2025); statewide active listings are down ~14% YoY as of end of June, and the median FL single-family price rose to $425,000 in May on a ninth straight month of sales gains.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Tightening inventory + price stabilization supports the FL real-estate thesis and points to a firmer demand backdrop for Brand 9's homebuilder clients (more signage/community-pipeline activity), even as affordability keeps a lid on volume growth.

## 3. Homebuilder consolidation wave closes out: Berkshire/Taylor Morrison and Sumitomo/Tri Pointe deals complete
- **Source:** https://www.housingwire.com/articles/berkshire-taylor-morrison-ecosystem/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/berkshire-taylor-morrison-bet-housing-market-bottom.html
- **What happened:** Berkshire Hathaway's $8.5B Taylor Morrison acquisition and Sumitomo Forestry's completed Tri Pointe Homes buyout ($47/share) cap a five-week spring 2026 stretch in which four U.S. homebuilders were bought by capital-patient conglomerates (Japanese housing groups plus Berkshire).
- **Hartley Capital implication:** Builder consolidation concentrates procurement at fewer, larger, more centralized HQs — a risk to watch for Brand 9's homebuilder-client signage relationships if acquirers push vendor consolidation, but also a potential wedge if The Construct/Brand 9 can land at the acquirer level and scale across the newly combined community pipelines.

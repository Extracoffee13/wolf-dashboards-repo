# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-09-15

- **[Florida housing rebalancing continues](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/)** — Statewide active inventory fell 12.8% YoY to 179,377 (July 2026), median single-family price at $425K (+3.7% YoY), homes averaging 47 days on market; 30-yr mortgage rate hit 6.76% on Sep 10, a 14-month high. *Implication:* Confirms the "stabilizing, not booming" FL housing thesis — supports a cautious-but-steady read on signage/build-out demand from homebuilder and commercial clients rather than a growth surge; insurance/affordability remains the drag to watch in Naples/Cape Coral/North Port specifically.

- **[Dream Finders Homes presses $2.2B Beazer Homes bid amid builder consolidation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/dream-finders-homes-boosts-beazer-bid-to-875-million); Lennar reports Q3 Sep 16-17 with margins under pressure ([Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260902la39275/lennar-corporation-to-broadcast-its-third-quarter-2026-earnings-call-on-september-17-2026))** — Jacksonville-HQ'd Dream Finders is escalating its takeover push for Beazer; Lennar (Brand 9's largest homebuilder relationship) reports Q3 earnings this week with consensus EPS down ~35% YoY and the stock off 44% from its 52-week high. *Implication:* Builder consolidation (Dream Finders/Beazer) could concentrate future signage/community-branding spend into fewer, larger accounts — a account-capture wedge worth flagging to sales; Lennar's margin compression is a leading indicator to watch on community-pipeline/signage budget commitments before betting further on that relationship this quarter.

- **[Enterprise AI-agent funding keeps accelerating](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/enterprise-ai-agent-funding-surges-093104627.html)** — $435M has poured into enterprise AI-agent security/governance startups since April 2026 (12 deals); empirik.ai (agent platform for infrastructure change) raised $21M in early September backed by Sequoia, S32, Canapi. *Implication:* Governance/security is where fresh agent-infra capital is concentrating, not general agent-ops — a data point for The Construct's own positioning and a signal that "safe to run in production" is becoming the differentiator competitors are funding toward.

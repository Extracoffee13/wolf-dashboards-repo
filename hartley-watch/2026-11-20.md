Lennar is still opening communities and delivering about 20.8K homes a quarter, so model-park and frontage signage volume should hold, but there will be heavy price pressure on vendors. Brand 9's Lennar revenue is durable but price-capped. Watch the Q4 call for any community-count cuts.

2. **Florida October: sales slowed on >7% mortgage rates, while single-family prices held and inventory fell year over year**
   - Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/fla-housing-market-higher-rates-160500499.html · https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/11/17/south-florida-housing-market-shows-resilience-amid-rising-rates/
   - What happened: Florida Realtors reported lower October sales. The single-family median rose year over year for the 8th straight month. Supply was 4.6 months for single-family homes and 8 months for condos. Active listings fell 7.4% (single-family) and 8.8% (condo). South Florida inventory was down 11% year over year for the 9th straight month. The 30-year rate was reported at ~7.4% in mid-October (tracker figure, not verified against Freddie Mac).
   - Hartley Capital implication: The resale market is tight and low-turnover, which pushes buyers toward new construction. Builders use rate buydowns to win them, which supports the builder-signage wedge over the resale/realtor-signage wedge. Condos at 8 months of supply are the weak spot, so avoid exposure tied to condos.

   - Source: https://fourweekmba.com/ai-linq-raises-90m-from-a16z-so-ai-agents-can-text-and-call/ · https://thenextweb.com/news/model-ml-250m-series-b-1-5bn-valuation-ai-agents-bankers

Smaller rounds in the same week: Metano ($12M, agent security), Unsiloed ($7.5M, agent-ready documents) and Pretxt ($5M, agent supervision).

Model ML's ARR curve shows vertical depth gets paid. Buying these layers is cheaper than building them.

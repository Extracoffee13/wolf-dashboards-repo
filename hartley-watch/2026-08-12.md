# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-08-12

- **Dream Finders Homes agrees to acquire Beazer Homes for $915M cash ($2.2B EV, incl. debt)** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/dream-finders-agrees-916-million-deal-for-beazer-homes) / [Jax Daily Record](https://www.jaxdailyrecord.com/news/2026/aug/07/dream-finders-buying-beazer-homes-in-22-billion-deal/)
  - What happened: Jacksonville-based Dream Finders will pay $33.50/share for Beazer, creating the 6th-largest U.S. homebuilder by revenue with ~520 active communities across 26 markets (Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Texas, West, Midwest); >$100M in targeted annual cost synergies; expected to close Q4 2026.
  - Hartley Capital implication: Directly validates the FL-homebuilder-client thesis — a Jacksonville hometown builder is about to roughly double its community pipeline, much of it Southeast-weighted. Worth mapping Beazer's existing FL/Southeast communities now for signage/wayfinding wedge opportunities ahead of the Q4 integration, before a competitor gets there first.

- **30-year mortgage rate dipped to 6.59% APR on a soft inflation print** — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/mortgages/news/mortgage-rates-today-wednesday-august-12-2026)
  - What happened: Rates eased from ~6.69% a week earlier after CPI came in with no surprises, though forecasters still expect rates to hold in the 6–7% band through the rest of 2026 (no return to 5%).
  - Hartley Capital implication: Modest relief, not a regime change — supports the "normalization, not recovery" read on FL housing (median prices flat YoY, ~84 days-on-market statewide) rather than a new demand catalyst; doesn't change urgency on any thesis.

- **Sapiom raises $35M Series A for AI agent deployment/ops infrastructure** — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/sapiom-raises-35-million-series-a-to-scale-ai-agent-infrastructure/)
  - What happened: Dragonfly-led round (Accel, Gradient, Coinbase Ventures, Operator Collective, Formus, VanEck Ventures participating) for a platform to deploy, operate, and scale AI agents — part of a broader surge in "agent execution infrastructure" funding (~21% of 2026 YTD agentic-AI deals).
  - Hartley Capital implication: Confirms agent-infra (runtimes, ops, control layers) is the hottest sub-category investors are chasing — a data point for The Construct's positioning; no direct competitive overlap in this round, but worth tracking whether Sapiom or similar move toward vertical/B2B agent-ops territory.

- **[Florida housing correction easing; mortgage rates dip to ~6.48%](https://www.resiclubanalytics.com/p/intensity-of-florida-housing-market-home-price-correction-easing-across-many-pockets)** (ResiClub Analytics / Close With Sun, early Aug 2026) — Statewide FL inventory sits at 4.5 months supply (below the 6-month "balanced" threshold) with active listings rising ~9% YoY as the mortgage lock-in effect fades; 30-year rates eased to ~6.48%. Regional divergence continues: Miami showing modest price gains (+1.1–3.7%), while Gulf Coast markets (Cape Coral, North Port) are still correcting down (-8.9% to -10.2%). Builders kept incentives elevated for a 14th straight month (61% of builders using them). **Implication:** softer Gulf Coast pricing (near Sarasota/Naples) plus persistent builder incentives is a headwind for near-term signage/community-branding demand from homebuilder clients in those submarkets, while Miami/SE Florida strength and easing rates support continued build-out — no thesis change, but reinforces watching Sarasota/Naples separately from the SE Florida book.

# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-09-21

- **Source:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/09/18/885681.htm
  **What happened:** Lennar's Q4 FY26 guidance (EPS $1.30–$1.65 vs. $1.99 consensus) came in soft as growing existing-home supply forces price cuts across its largest markets — Florida and Texas; 38% of builders nationally are now cutting prices (avg. 6%), up from 35% in August.

Validates caution on any exposure sized to new-construction signage growth.

  **Hartley Capital implication:** Confirms the slowdown is market-wide, not Lennar-specific — strengthens the case for diversifying signage revenue beyond new-home builder clients rather than treating the current softness as a one-off.

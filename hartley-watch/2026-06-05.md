**1. Florida RE market hits inflection point; inventory tightens to 4.7 months**

- **Source:** [Florida housing market at inflection point as summer approaches — Florida Realtors](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/05/florida-housing-market-inflection-point-summer-approaches)
- **What happened:** Statewide inventory fell to a 4.7-month supply (below the 6-month balanced threshold) as of May 2026. Median SFH price is $420K, +1.8% YoY — just 2.3% under the April 2024 all-time high. Correctly priced homes in Jacksonville, Orlando, Tampa, Miami, Sarasota, and Naples are selling in ~44 days with occasional multi-offer situations. Jacksonville notably remains the highest-supply submarket and is tracking toward balance by 2027.
- **Hartley Capital implication:** The "FL buyer's market" window that opened in 2024–25 is closing faster than consensus expected. Any thesis involving entry into FL residential land, BTR, or homebuilder-adjacent services should treat this as a tightening window; Jacksonville remains the most actionable submarket for value plays before it re-equilibrates.

# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-10-29

1. **Florida market: Q3/September steady but softening** — https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/10/florida-housing-market-holds-steady-september
   - What happened: Florida Realtors' September/Q3 data show prices flat, a second straight month of YoY sales declines on higher rates, inventory tighter YoY, luxury strong; Q3 single-family median -1.2% YoY, condo/townhouse -6.1% (the condo figure is from a secondary source, https://www.mpamag.com/us/mortgage-industry/market-updates/florida-home-sales-rebound-as-prices-stabilize/554436, which also carries some 2025 material).
   - Hartley implication: Validates a thesis we're considering: a bifurcated FL market (strong high-end, weak condo) favors new-build and master-planned communities over condo exposure. It is also a small support for community-signage demand.

Horton's July guidance was cut and its Q3 cancellation rate was 20%; its Q4 release is reportedly due 10/29 (sources disagree on the date). No Florida-specific guidance was found, and the Pulte item is stale.

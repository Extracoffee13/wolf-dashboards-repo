# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-21

- **D.R. Horton (NYSE:DHI) fiscal Q3 2026 earnings — homebuilder client signal**
  Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/d-r-horton-q3-earnings-140650881.html
  What happened: DHI beat Q3 estimates (EPS $3.20, revenue $9.2B, 20.7% gross margin) but cut FY26 guidance — revenue to $32.5–33.0B (from $33.5–34.5B) and closings to 83,800–84,300 homes — citing persistent affordability pressure, cautious buyers, and incentives staying elevated through Q4.
  Hartley Capital implication: Validates the "builder marketing spend stays incentive-driven, not community-count-driven" thesis for FL-exposed homebuilder clients — expect steady signage/POS demand tied to move-in-ready inventory pushes rather than new-community expansion in the near term.

- **Neo Security — $100M stealth exit for agentic-AI control layer**
  Source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/20/neo-security-bags-100m-build-secure-control-layer-enterprise-ai-agents/
  What happened: Neo (ex-SentinelOne/Wiz/Palo Alto Networks team) raised a $100M seed/Series A led by a16z and Bessemer to build inventory, posture, and policy control for enterprise AI agents, citing Gartner's forecast that 40%+ of enterprise apps will carry agentic features by year-end 2026.
  Hartley Capital implication: Confirms capital is now flowing into the "agent ops/governance" layer, not just agent-building — a wedge worth watching for The Construct's competitive positioning, and a reminder that well-funded entrants are moving fast into adjacent agent-infra territory.

- **Florida Realtors 2Q 2026 housing report — statewide real estate signal**
  Source: https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/07/florida-inventory-tells-split-market-story
  What happened: Statewide single-family median price hit $425K in 2Q26 (+2.4% YoY) with closed and pending sales up YoY; inventory sits at ~4.7 months supply (below the ~6-month balanced mark), with Orlando/Lakeland carrying the largest surpluses and Miami still under pre-pandemic norms — Florida Realtors characterizes the market as "stabilizing, not crashing."
  Hartley Capital implication: No thesis change — demand backdrop remains steady for FL builder/signage clients, but the ~$8K/yr insurance-cost burden and new condo reserve rules (effective Jan 1, 2026) remain a drag worth tracking in the Miami/Naples condo segment specifically.

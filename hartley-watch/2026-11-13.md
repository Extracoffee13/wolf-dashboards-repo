- **AI agent-ops infrastructure keeps raising money: Lumbra, $25M Series A (Nov 10, 2026)**
  - Source: https://www.tectonicdefense.com/exclusive-agentic-ai-startup-lumbra-raises-25m-series-a/
  - What happened: Point72 Ventures and Ansa Capital co-led the round. LumbraOS is a model-neutral "operating system for agents" already running on Pentagon and IC networks, and it is now expanding into finance, healthcare and legal. The same week, Inkbox raised a $5.3M seed for persistent cloud agents (https://cryptobriefing.com/inkbox-raises-5-million-cloud-ai-agents/).
  - Hartley Capital implication: This supports the agent-ops / orchestration-layer thesis behind The Construct. It also shows funded horizontal players heading for regulated verticals. The Construct's defensible wedge is vertical depth (trades, signage and SMB ops), not general agent infrastructure.

- **Mortgage rates eased for a third straight week: 30-yr at 7.18% (Freddie Mac, Nov 5, 2026)**
  - Source: https://www.rismedia.com/2026/11/05/mortgage-rates-edge-down-third-straight-week/
  - What happened: The 30-yr fixed fell from 7.32% (Oct 22) to 7.24% and then 7.18%. That is still about 100 bp above a year ago (6.22%). Florida inventory remains above pre-pandemic norms in every major metro except Miami (Orlando is about +41%), and prices are forecast flat into 2027.
  - Hartley Capital implication: The pressure on builder margins (Lennar GM about 15.8%, FY26 deliveries cut to 80–81k) eases only slightly. Expect builders to keep leaning on incentives and model-park merchandising, which supports Brand 9's community-signage revenue. FL real-estate acquisition theses should keep underwriting flat prices.

- **Sign-industry roll-up: Kirkwood buys Cadwell Design + Sign (Oct 19, 2026; most recent deal found)**
  - Source: https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/kirkwood-acquires-cadwell-designsign/
  - What happened: Marketing-execution provider Kirkwood bought a custom signage and environmental-graphics shop in Massachusetts, its 9th acquisition in 15 years. The whole team was retained.
  - Hartley Capital implication: Strategic buyers are still picking up founder-owned sign shops that have design and EGD capability. That keeps an exit or comp path open for Brand 9 and shows that Brand 9's platform plus vertical client book (homebuilders) is the kind of asset that gets rolled up.

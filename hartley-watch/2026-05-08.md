## 3. Gulf Coast Florida price declines deepen; Gulf-to-Coast divergence widens (ongoing, May 2026)

**Source:** [Pegasus Lends — Is the Florida Housing Market Crashing in 2026?](https://pegasuslends.com/blog/is-the-florida-housing-market-crashing-in-2026-the-truth/) | [Norada Real Estate — Florida Housing Market Forecast](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/)

**What happened:** Naples/Sarasota and the broader Gulf Coast corridor is down 7–9% YoY on post-hurricane insurance cost spikes and condo reserve mandates. Tampa Case-Shiller is off 3.9% YoY. By contrast, Jacksonville holds ~4.5 months supply (resilient), Orlando is forecast +1.2%, and Miami is flat-to-up on cash/international demand.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Any Florida real estate concentration in SW Gulf metros warrants re-underwriting; Jacksonville and Orlando remain defensible holds. The inter-market divergence is a potential wedge — distressed Gulf Coast inventory or builder lot positions could be attractive at a discount if insurance/reserve headwinds are transitory rather than structural.

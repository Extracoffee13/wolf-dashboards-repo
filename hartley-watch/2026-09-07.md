# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-09-07

- **Florida mortgage rates elevated, inventory rising, prices flatlining.** As of late August 2026, Florida's 30-year fixed mortgage APR sits near 7.04% (Redfin/movewithmomentum.com trackers), statewide home prices went flat in July with ~150,000 active listings and 75% of ZIPs still down YoY. *Hartley implication: confirms the affordability-ceiling thesis — buyer demand stays rate-suppressed even as supply loosens, which caps near-term upside for any FL housing-adjacent position and keeps homebuilder community-pipeline growth (below) more volume-driven than price-driven.*
  Source: https://movewithmomentum.com/data/florida-housing-tracker

- **Sign-industry PE roll-ups are now the default strategy across the category.** Trade coverage of the ~$34B fragmented US sign market shows every major regional integrator actively consolidating — Propelled Brands/Lariat Partners (FastSigns), Alliance Franchise Brands (Signs Now, Image360, ID Plus), and United Franchise Group (Signarama) — with recurring service-contract revenue now valued over fabrication backlog. *Hartley implication: validates the roll-up thesis for the signage vertical and signals rising competitive/acquisition pressure on independent shops like Brand 9 — worth tracking whether any of these platforms are acquisitive in the FL market specifically.*
  Source: https://ctacquisitions.com/private-equity-roll-up-strategy/

- **Capital keeps flowing into the agent-ops/agent-governance layer.** Zenity, an AI-agent security and governance platform, raised $125M (~Aug 2, 2026); HappyRobot closed a $150M Series C at a $1.2B post-money valuation (~Aug 4, 2026), led by Prysm Capital. *Hartley implication: the governance/ops layer around agents is attracting large late-stage checks, which is adjacent to but distinct from The Construct's build-your-own-agent positioning — a wedge may exist in packaging governance/ops as a feature rather than ceding that layer to a separate vendor.*
  Source: https://www.dutchstartup.ai/en/news/ai-agent-platforms-raise-tens-of-millions-as-enterprise-adoption-accelerates

# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-11-11

How this was run: a web scan of the past 24 hours. Little turned up in that window. The newest Florida Realtors data is for September (the October release is due Nov 16). No signage-franchise roll-up deal turned up for Propelled Brands, Image360, Stratus or Allegra. No Lennar or other client news turned up after the Sept 16 Q3 print. Each bullet gives its own date, and two of the three are older than 24h.

- **Nous Research raises a $90M Series B to launch "Hermes for Businesses" (reported ~Nov 5; Rox's $100M Series B at a $1.3B valuation for sales-lifecycle agents is also out this week)**
  - Source: https://tech-insider.org/nous-research-90-million-series-b-ai-agents-2026/ (Rox: https://www.artiverse.ca/roxs-13b-bet-puts-ai-agents-on-a-bigger-enterprise-mission/)
  - What happened: Nous is selling a private, customizable enterprise version of Hermes for multi-step workflow agents. Its total funding is now reported at $158M.
  - Hartley Capital implication: This touches The Construct directly, because Hermes is its execution layer. A well-funded vendor going upmarket means a better-supported base (good). It also means "agents on Hermes" stops being a wedge (bad). That pushes The Construct's moat onto its vertical playbooks (signage, homebuilder ops, Gatekeeper), not the agent runtime. Check whether the Hermes licence or pricing changes.

- **Navori Labs buys Carousel Digital Signage from Tightrope Media (Nov 9)**
  - Source: https://invidis.com/news/2026/11/ma-navori-acquires-carousel-digital-signage/
  - What happened: Navori bought Carousel, a Minneapolis digital-signage CMS with more than 1,100 customers in higher ed, government and corporate. It is Navori's second major add-on, after Signagelive in Aug 2025.
  - Hartley Capital implication: Digital-signage software is consolidating, but physical-sign franchise roll-ups have gone quiet. This supports a "software layer is being rolled up; fabrication/install is still fragmented" view. The open wedge for Brand 9 is still physical builder and community signage, not CMS.

- **Florida demand is softening even as rates ease: the 30-yr fixed fell to 7.18% (Freddie Mac, Nov 5, a third straight weekly drop), Redfin shows FL pending sales down 16.4% YoY, and Lennar is choosing volume over margin**
  - Source: https://www.rismedia.com/2026/11/05/mortgage-rates-edge-down-third-straight-week/ ; https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/10/florida-housing-market-holds-steady-september ; https://www.builderonline.com/?p=177394
  - What happened: In September, FL months of supply was 4.4 (5.1 a year earlier) and the median price was $415K (+1.2%). But pending sales are falling. Lennar's guidance is Q4 GM of 15.5–16% and FY26 deliveries of 80–81K (cut), with FY results due in December.
  - Hartley Capital implication: Builders that push volume through incentives keep opening and merchandising communities, which supports Brand 9's signage revenue in the near term. Margin pressure means price pushback on sign packages, though. Hold the thesis but expect lower ticket sizes. Watch the Nov 16 FL Realtors print and the Lennar Q4 call in December for any cut to community count.

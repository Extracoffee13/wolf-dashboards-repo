## Bullet 2 — AI / The Construct: Model Providers Vertically Integrating Into Agent Deployment

**Source:** [AI Infrastructure Investment: May 2026 Capital Flows — Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-infrastructure-investment-may-2026-capital)

**What happened:** Within 17 days, both leading U.S. model providers stood up enterprise agent deployment vehicles. Anthropic closed a $1.5B JV with Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and Hellman & Friedman (May 4). OpenAI launched its "Deployment Company" — a $4B+ professional services vehicle backed by TPG (May 11) — targeting direct enterprise AI agent deployment. Agentic AI startup average round size has now reached $155M, double the H1 2025 pace.

**Hartley Capital implication:** Direct competitive pressure on The Construct's positioning. When foundational model companies launch professional services arms, pure-play agent platforms face margin compression and customer acquisition headwinds. The Construct's defensible path is vertical specialization (e.g., signage ops, homebuilder workflows) before incumbents commoditize the horizontal deployment layer. This compresses the differentiation window — a hold/accelerate decision point.

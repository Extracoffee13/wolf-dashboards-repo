1. **Sign-industry roll-up: Ameribrands buys Illumatech Signs (announced Nov 17, closed Oct 14)**
   - Source: https://www.tmcnet.com/usubmit/2026/11/17/10483087.htm (also https://www.citybiz.co/article/934807/ameribrands-acquires-illumatech-signs-to-expand-national-signage-and-branded-environment-services/)
   - What happened: Georgia holding company Ameribrands bought Louisiana sign maker Illumatech. It is Ameribrands' 4th deal of 2026 and 5th since 2023, built around "national signage + branded environment" services.
   - Hartley Capital implication: This supports the sign roll-up thesis. A new Southeast platform buyer, besides the PE-backed franchisors, is paying for regional fabricators with national-account reach. That sets a near-term comparable for what a Gulf Coast/Florida shop with homebuilder accounts (Brand 9's profile) could be worth, either as a target or as a bolt-on.

2. **Rates and Florida demand: 30-yr fixed at 7.28% (Freddie Mac, Nov 19); Florida's October pending sales down 8.1% YoY**
   - Source: https://www.streetinsider.com/Globe+Newswire/Mortgage+Rates+Average+7.28%25/27375239.html ; https://www.floridarealtors.org/newsroom/flas-housing-market-higher-rates-slow-oct-sales-prices-mixed
   - What happened: Mortgage rates rose to 7.28%, from 7.11% the week before and 6.26% a year ago. Florida single-family inventory was 98.7K (4.6 months of supply). The median price was $629K (+3.6%), but pending sales fell 8.1%. Lennar's Q3 commentary said conditions had "deteriorated", and D.R. Horton cut its FY26 closings guidance.
   - Hartley Capital implication: This is a headwind to any thesis that relies on growth in builder signage volume. Slower sales mean fewer new community launches but more incentive and "move-in ready" marketing signage, which is a mix shift toward fast-turn promotional signs rather than new model-park installs. Watch Lennar's Q4 report in mid-December for changes to the community count.

3. **Agent-ops funding: Traversal raises $120M Series B at a ~$1B valuation (Nov 3; per tracker, not yet confirmed by a second source)**
   - Source: https://zplatform.ai/guides/ai-agent-startups-funding/
   - What happened: Khosla, Kleiner Perkins and Redpoint backed an AI-SRE/agent-operations company. It is one of about 30 agent-startup rounds the tracker logged in 2026 (others include Sycamore's $65M seed, Skan's $63M and Gradial's $65M).
   - Hartley Capital implication: Agent infrastructure and ops for horizontal enterprise use is getting crowded and expensive, so The Construct should not compete there. Its defensible position is vertical: agents run inside a real operating SMB (signage and homebuilder fulfillment), proven by live revenue, which the infrastructure players don't have.

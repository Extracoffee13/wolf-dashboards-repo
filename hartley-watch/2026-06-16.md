## 2. Alliance Franchise Brands executing local roll-up: Allegra acquires Image360 franchise unit

**Hartley Capital implication:** AFB is actively consolidating franchise units across its Allegra/Image360/Signs Now portfolio, compressing owner-operator independence at the local level. A Hartley position in an independent regional signage operator faces direct competitive pressure as consolidated AFB units gain shared back-office and purchasing scale. Conversely, this validates a wedge: tech-enabled independents (via The Construct's agent tooling) can match AFB's operational efficiency without surrendering equity.

- **Source:** [Assumable mortgages with rates in the 2% range can help buyers afford more home despite higher interest rates — News4JAX](https://www.news4jax.com/news/local/2026/07/02/assumable-mortgages-with-rates-in-the-2-range-can-help-buyers-afford-more-home-despite-higher-interest-rates/)
  **What happened:** FL 30-yr fixed sits at 6.25% (national avg 6.43%, down slightly WoW); Jacksonville buyers are increasingly hunting assumable mortgages in the 2–3% range to sidestep current rates, while Jacksonville inventory has normalized to ~4-month supply and prices are roughly flat YoY.
  **Hartley implication:** Confirms the FL housing thesis — rate relief is marginal and buyers are engineering workarounds rather than transacting at market rates, which points to continued soft, buyer-patient demand in Brand 9's core builder markets through summer 2026. Worth flagging to homebuilder clients as a reason to lean into financing-incentive marketing rather than price cuts.

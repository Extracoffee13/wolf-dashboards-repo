# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-11-18

Scope note: none of the watched categories had a confirmed item from the last 24h. The three items below are the freshest relevant signals, all from Nov 10–13, 2026. 1. **Florida resale volume is contracting while prices hold**
   - Source: https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/11/florida-home-sales-slow-october-mortgage-rates-rise (rates: https://themortgagereports.com/mortgage-rates-now/mortgage-rates-today-november-6-2026)
   - What happened: October FL single-family closed sales fell 10.1% YoY and new pendings fell 15.7%. Active listings fell 7.4% to 98.7k, while the median price rose 2.2% to $420k (8th straight YoY gain). Condo median fell 3.3% to $290k. Freddie Mac's 30-yr was 7.11% on Nov 12, with the 10-yr Treasury around 5.1%.
   - Hartley Capital implication: This affects the builder-client thesis. With resale volume down and rates above 7%, builders keep leaning on incentives and volume over margin (Lennar's stated stance). For Brand 9 that means more community launches and model-park signage per closing, but also tighter pricing pressure from builders. Condo weakness argues against condo-heavy wedges (Miami/Naples).

2. **Rox raised a $100M Series B at a $1.3B valuation for agentic sales agents (Nov 11)**
   - Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/rox-raises-100m-series-b/
   - What happened: General Catalyst and March Capital co-led, with Sequoia and GV returning. Rox's agents prospect, book meetings and manage deals using the customer's own data warehouse data, pitched as a replacement for CRM-centric workflows at Global 2000 companies.
   - Hartley Capital implication: This validates the agent-ops thesis behind The Construct, and also shows that the enterprise horizontal sales-agent lane is well funded and crowded. The Construct's defensible wedge stays vertical: SMB and trades operators, signage and builder workflows with physical fulfillment. Don't compete head-on for G2000 sales agents.

3. **Digital-signage software is consolidating under PE: Navori Labs (backed by Maguar Capital) is buying Carousel Digital Signage (Nov 10)**
   - Source: https://www.hlc.com/en/news/hogan-lovells-cadwalader-advises-maguar-capital-and-navori-labs-on-the-acquisition-of-carousel-digital-signage
   - What happened: Navori is buying Carousel (Minneapolis, 1,100+ customers, 30 staff) from Tightrope Media Systems. It is Navori's second CMS add-on, after Signagelive in 2025.
   - Hartley Capital implication: This could be a new wedge. PE roll-ups are active in signage software but quiet in physical sign franchises this cycle. That leaves a gap for a sign shop that adds an AI/software layer (Brand 9 plus Construct) and could become an acquirer, or an attractive target, rather than a roll-up casualty.

**2. Florida condo glut deepens while SFH supply stays tight**

- **Source:** [Redfin Florida Market](https://www.redfin.com/state/Florida/housing-market) · [Heart Mortgage Blog](https://blog.heartmortgage.com/post/2026-florida-housing-market-trends-2025-2026-median-home-price-inventory-days-on-market) · [Norada Real Estate](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/florida-housing-market-forecast-for-next-2-years/)
- **What happened:** As of May 2026, statewide condo inventory hit 9.7 months of supply vs. 5.2 months for SFH. Jacksonville is back to a 4-month SFH supply (resilient). Tampa Bay SFH median holds at ~$400K for 24+ months; 3.8-month supply — still below equilibrium. Miami SFH price growth 1.1–2.2% annually; Orlando forecasting +1.2% by year-end.
- **Hartley implication:** The bifurcation confirms the thesis: new-construction SFH (homebuilder clients) is the right lane — condo/resale overhang is a headwind for competing asset owners, not builders. Watch Naples/Sarasota coastal condo exposure in any RE-adjacent portfolio; distressed condo deal flow may become a wedge opportunity in H2.

The wedge: any B2B agent play must show infrastructure-layer defensibility (workflow ownership, compliance hooks, cross-system coordination) to compete for the next raise. Surface this in any The Construct diligence or portfolio review.

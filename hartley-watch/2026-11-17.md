# Hartley Capital Watch: 2026-11-17

Window: the 24 hours to 2026-11-17. Items from outside that window are labeled.

- **Florida resale sales are slowing while prices hold, and pendings are falling faster than closings.**
  Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/flas-housing-market-higher-rates-slow-oct-sales-prices-mixed-302940636.html (also https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/11/florida-home-sales-slow-october-mortgage-rates-rise and https://therealdeal.com/miami/2026/11/16/south-florida-home-sales-drop-in-october-2026/)
  - What happened: Florida Realtors' October report came out on Nov 16. Single-family closings were 19,049, down 10.1% YoY. The single-family median was $420K, up 2.2%, the 8th straight month of YoY gains. The condo median was $290K, down 3.3%. New pending sales fell 15.7% for single-family and 11.9% for condos. Active listings were 61,983, down 8.8%. The 30-year mortgage rate (Freddie Mac) was 7.11% on Nov 12, down four weeks in a row from 7.40% but about 87 bp higher than a year ago.
  - Hartley Capital implication: This supports the "FL volume down, price sticky" thesis. Builders have to fight harder for each sale, which means more incentives and more on-site marketing. That favors Brand 9's model-park and community-frontage signage spend over land or lot plays. The 15.7% drop in pendings points to weaker Q1 2027 closings. Hold off on any FL resale-exposed position. The demand wedge for builder marketing gets stronger.

- **A sign-industry roll-up made another add-on: Ameribrands bought Illumatech Signs.**
  Source: https://accessnorthga.com/ap_article/ameribrands-acquires-illumatech-signs-expanding-national-signage-and-branded-environment-capabilities (Business Wire, 2026-11-17)
  - What happened: Ameribrands announced the acquisition of Illumatech Signs, a national provider covering design, engineering, fabrication, installation and maintenance. The deal closed on Oct 14. It is Ameribrands' 4th or 5th acquisition of 2026, after Randal Retail Group in July. SouthState Bank financed earlier deals. The sponsor is not named in any source I found.
  - Hartley Capital implication: This validates the buy-and-build thesis in national multi-site signage (design + fab + install). Roll-ups are paying for full-stack capability, and Brand 9 already has that stack. It also sets a comparable for B9's own exit or partnership options. Track the multiples Ameribrands pays. Nothing new was found on Propelled Brands (FASTSIGNS), Image360 or Allegra.

- **Money keeps going into the agent-infra and governance layer (prior week, Nov 7-13, outside the 24h window).**
  Source: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/ and https://casrai.org/news/bay-thirty-seven-million-series-a-agent-security-2026
  - What happened: LangChain reportedly raised a $300M Series C at a $3B valuation. Bay raised a $37M Series A for agent security (led by Battery). Graphon Labs raised $35M for agent memory and data infra. WhiteMatter raised $20M for agent governance. Figures come from secondary coverage and were not checked against the primary announcements.
  - Hartley Capital implication: Generic agent ops and infra is now well funded and crowded. The Construct should not compete on horizontal infra. Its defensible wedge is vertical agents running real operating businesses (sign shop and builder workflows), plus the proof-of-operation data they generate.

Not surfaced: no new Lennar or other homebuilder-client moves in the window. Lennar's last report was FQ3 on Sep 16, when it cut FY deliveries to 80-81K and guided Q4 gross margin to 15.5-16.0%. FQ4 is expected mid-December. No notable signage-tech filings were found.

Scope: FL real estate · sign-industry M&A / PE roll-ups · agent-AI fundraises · Brand 9 homebuilder clients · signage-tech trade press.

**Coverage note:** The searches found nothing dated inside the last 24 hours. The three items below are the most recent relevant ones, from the past 3–8 days. 1. **Florida inventory is shrinking while mortgage rates stay above 7%.**
   - Sources: https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/October-2026-Fla-single-family-summary.pdf · https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms
   - What happened: Florida Realtors released its October data on Nov 16. Single-family supply was 4.6 months, down from 5.1 a year earlier. Median days on market fell to 47 from 54, with about 98.7k active listings. Freddie Mac's 30-year rate was 7.11% on Nov 12, down from 7.18% but still about 90 bps above last year.
   - Hartley implication: This supports the thesis that Florida is moving from a glut back toward balance. Supply tightened even with rates above 7%, so builders are likely to keep opening communities, and that drives demand for model-park and frontage signage. Orlando and Lakeland had the largest surpluses and remain the softest. JAX, Tampa and Gulf Coast are the better places for Brand 9 to push.

2. **Plurai raised a $13M seed (Nov 11) for agent simulation and pre-deployment testing.**
   - Source: https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/bkhfcabame
   - What happened: The round was led by Team8, and Plurai's platform is now generally available. It builds a digital replica of an agent and its environment, then runs thousands of adversarial scenarios to catch hallucinations and policy violations. Plato Technologies raised $16M (Nov 2) for a similar sandbox-testing approach.
   - Hartley implication: The money is going to agent *ops and reliability*, not to more agent builders. This supports The Construct's verify-before-claiming and gate approach as a selling point. It also means a funded competitor in agent QA. The Construct should treat evals and gates as table stakes and compete on vertical, operator-run outcomes instead of horizontal testing tools.

3. **Signage consolidation continues, but in the adjacent segments, not franchise print/sign.**
   - Sources: https://www.valleynewslive.com/2026/11/12/newman-signs-outdoor-advertising-division-has-been-sold/ · https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/kirkwood-acquires-cadwell-designsign/
   - What happened: Lamar bought Newman Outdoor's billboard assets (Nov 11), about 2,250 faces including 73 digital. Kirkwood bought Cadwell Design + Sign, a custom signage and environmental graphics firm (Oct 19). Navori bought Carousel's digital-signage CMS (Nov 9).
   - Hartley implication: Buyers are paying for independent custom-fabrication shops (Kirkwood/Cadwell) and for recurring-revenue assets. This is a potential new wedge. Brand 9's builder-program revenue and agent-run online shop could make it either an acquirer or a premium target. Track the multiples on custom-fab deals.

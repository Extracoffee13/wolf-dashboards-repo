# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-11-12

Window note: only the mortgage-rate item falls strictly inside the last 24h. The other two are 48–72h old and are included because nothing fresher turned up. No new items surfaced for Propelled Brands, Image360, Stratus or Allegra, and there was no Lennar or homebuilder-client news this week. The last Lennar item is Q3 FY26: FY deliveries cut to 80–81k and Q4 gross margin guided to 15.5–16%. Florida Realtors' October data comes out Mon 11/16.

1. **Mortgage rates: four weekly declines in a row, then a hot-CPI reversal**
   - Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/12/3402013/0/en/mortgage-rates-decline-for-fourth-consecutive-week-to-7-11.html (daily reversal: https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/mortgages/article/mortgage-rates-are-moving-higher-after-inflation-data-came-in-hot-mortgage-and-refinance-interest-rates-today-thursday-november-12-2026-173837898.html)
   - What happened: Freddie Mac's 30-yr fixed fell to 7.11%, its fourth straight weekly drop (6.24% a year ago). On the same day, MND's daily rate jumped 12bp to 7.42% after October CPI came in hot.
   - Hartley implication: this supports the "FL builders trade margin for volume" thesis. Rates above 7% plus Orlando and Lakeland inventory surpluses mean more incentives and more community launches pushing to move units. That is demand for model-park and frontage signage even while builder margins compress. Watch for Lennar's Q4 print in mid-December to cut signage budgets per community.

2. **Navori acquires Carousel Digital Signage (carve-out from Tightrope Media)**
   - Source: https://invidis.com/news/2026/11/ma-navori-acquires-carousel-digital-signage/
   - What happened: Swiss CMS vendor Navori bought Carousel, which has 1,100+ organizations and 45k+ displays. It is Navori's second roll-up after Signagelive.
   - Hartley implication: consolidation is happening in digital-signage software, not in the print and fabricated franchise layer (FastSigns, Image360 and similar are quiet). This supports holding the physical community-signage wedge as unconsolidated. A digital and agent-managed signage layer on top of it would be a possible new wedge.

3. **Cluster of agent-infrastructure raises (Nov 10)**
   - Source: https://cryptobriefing.com/graphon-raises-35m-ai-agent-memory/ (also: Lumbra $25M Series A, Point72/Ansa; Inkbox $5.3M, Peak XV, https://cryptobriefing.com/inkbox-raises-5-million-cloud-ai-agents/)
   - What happened: Graphon raised a $35M Series A for persistent agent memory, Inkbox raised for cloud agents with persistent computers, and Lumbra raised for a controlled agent platform. Amounts come from secondary sources and are unverified.
   - Hartley implication: agent memory and runtime are becoming funded commodity infrastructure. The Construct's defensible position is the vertical operating playbooks (signage, homebuilder, estate), not the infrastructure. Build on these layers rather than compete with them.

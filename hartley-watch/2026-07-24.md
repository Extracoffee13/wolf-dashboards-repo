# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-24

- **Source:** [America's Largest Homebuilder Says Buyers Are Still Hesitating — Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/07/60574774/americas-largest-homebuilder-says-buyers-are-still-hesitating)
  **What happened:** D.R. Horton beat Q3 FY26 earnings estimates but cut full-year guidance — revenue outlook trimmed to $32.5–33.0B (from $33.5–34.5B) and closings cut to 83,800–84,300 homes (from 86,000–87,500), citing persistent buyer hesitation on affordability.
  **Hartley Capital implication:** Directly touches a Brand 9 homebuilder-client thesis — a top-tier national builder pulling back closings guidance signals softer community-opening pace, which flows through to signage/wayfinding order volume for builder clients over the next 2-3 quarters. Worth flagging as a demand-side headwind to watch, not yet a thesis-breaker.

- **Source:** [Florida Realtors — New construction tells different stories across Florida](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/07/new-construction-tells-different-stories-across-florida)
  **What happened:** Four of the five U.S. metros with the steepest YoY price drops (Jan 2025–Jan 2026) are in Southwest Florida: Punta Gorda (-11.9%), Cape Coral–Fort Myers (-9.2%), North Port–Sarasota–Bradenton (-7.5%), and Naples–Marco Island (-6.0%) — while Jacksonville and Tampa still show tight inventory and YoY price gains.
  **Hartley Capital implication:** Confirms a bifurcated FL market — Sarasota/Naples softness validates caution on any near-term SW Florida signage/real-estate-adjacent bet, while Jacksonville/Tampa tightness (inventory down ~39% YoY in Jax, sub-4-month supply in Tampa) supports staying overweight those two metros for now.

- **Source:** [Venture Capital & Startup Funding Roundup, July 6, 2026 — TechStartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/06/venture-capital-startup-funding-roundup-july-6-2026/)
  **What happened:** AI agent startups pulled in ~$1.8B across 12+ deals in July, with "agent infrastructure" (marketplaces, deployment, monitoring, security, evaluation) called out as the fastest-growing sub-category and average valuations up ~40% QoQ to ~$280M; Bespoke Labs raised a $40M Series A specifically for agent training/eval environments.
  **Hartley Capital implication:** Capital is chasing agent *infrastructure/ops* tooling, not just vertical agents — a wedge The Construct should watch since well-funded infra entrants could commoditize pieces of the agent-ops layer The Construct is building on. No direct competitor named yet, but the category is heating up fast.

# Hartley Capital Watch — 2026-07-01

- **Florida homebuilders keep leaning into SW FL / Tampa Bay corridor while national numbers soften.** Taylor Morrison named a new Sarasota division president tied to two communities bringing 2,000+ homes online there in the next year, and Mattamy Homes closed on 145 homesites in Pasco County (Tampa) on June 5, even as Lennar's Q2 orders fell 4% YoY and D.R. Horton narrowed FY26 revenue guidance to $33.5–34.5B on affordability pressure (30-yr FL mortgage ~6.88%, inventory up ~9% annually, 3.8–4.6 months supply in Tampa/Jacksonville/Broward).
  Source: https://www.businessobserverfl.com/news/2026/jun/16/homebuilder-sarasota-president/ ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mattamy-homes-expands-tampa-presence-with-land-acquisition-in-pasco-county-302812853.html
  Implication: Validates the thesis that SW FL/Tampa Bay remains the active new-community pipeline for Brand9's homebuilder clients even as national builder guidance softens — signage/wayfinding demand should stay concentrated there, not spread evenly across the state; DR Horton/Lennar guidance is a leading indicator worth tracking for client budget pressure next quarter.

- **Sign-industry roll-up activity continues via independent operators, not just PE platforms.** ID Signsystems (architectural signage/wayfinding) was acquired in May by an independent growth operator rather than a PE-backed platform, while market commentary confirms regional PE-backed sign integrators are still actively rolling up multi-shop operators at 3x–8x EBITDA (platform-grade 5x–8x, national integrators 7x–10x).
  Source: https://rbj.net/2026/05/12/id-signsystems-announces-acquisition-to-accelerate-growth-and-build-a-signage-platform/
  Implication: Confirms the sign-industry consolidation thesis is still live and valuations are holding — no new FastSigns/Propelled Brands or Image360-parent (Alliance) deal surfaced in the last 24h, but the multiple environment supports Brand9's own roll-up/exit optionality if pursued.

- **Agent-security/governance layer is where fresh capital is landing, not raw agent-execution platforms.** Straiker raised a $64M Series A (led by Marathon, with Citi Ventures, Illuminate Financial, Workday Ventures, Bain Capital Ventures, Lightspeed) to secure and govern enterprise AI agents, bringing its total to $85M.
  Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/straiker-64m-series-a-agentic-security
  Implication: Signals a governance/trust wedge adjacent to The Construct rather than a direct competitor — worth watching whether enterprise buyers start requiring a security/governance layer bundled with agent-ops platforms, which could become a partnership or feature-parity requirement rather than a threat.

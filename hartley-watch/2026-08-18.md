- **Dream Finders Homes to acquire Beazer Homes for ~$2.2B** (announced Aug 7, 2026). Source: https://www.builderonline.com/money/ma/dream-finders-homes-to-acquire-beazer-homes-for-2-2-billion/

  Hartley Capital implication: Validates the thesis that FL-anchored builders are consolidating and scaling community counts — a tailwind for signage/wayfinding demand if Dream Finders stays a client; also a risk window (vendor/spec disruption) during integration worth tracking as the close date approaches.

  Hartley Capital implication: Confirms serious capital is flowing into horizontal agent-governance/ops tooling — a category The Construct should avoid competing in directly; reinforces the wedge should stay vertical (signage/local-market agent applications) rather than horizontal agent infra.

- **Tampa Bay single-family housing: tightening supply, rising concessions** (data as of late July 2026). Source: https://nowtb.com/florida-housing-market-2026/
  What happened: Tampa Bay median SF home price ~$424K with inventory at a tight 3.8-month supply; homes sitting longer with more price reductions and seller concessions as negotiating leverage shifts.
  Hartley Capital implication: Signals a cooling-but-still-constrained Tampa market — near-term new-construction signage volume in that metro may soften slightly even as builders keep incentivizing to move product; worth revisiting alongside Jacksonville/Orlando data next cycle.
